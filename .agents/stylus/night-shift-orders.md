## New tasks (add to `tasks.md` as section F, same format, owners Muse unless said)

- **F1. View tables like the shadcn data table example.** Records table: consistent cell padding (no content flush to an edge, including the checkbox and row-actions columns), header and cells share one alignment per column (text left, numbers and money right with tabular figures, dates left, image thumbnails fixed size and vertically centered), hairline row separators, the actions column narrow and right-aligned, no empty filler column stretching after the last field, header labels from field labels (not keys, so "Image", not "image"), selection count footer ("0 of 5 selected"). Check 375, 768, 1280, 1440 in both themes. Follow `impeccable` and `emilkowalski-motion`.
- **F2. Type to filter.** The search box filters as the user types from the first character (debounced about 150 ms, server-side text match over the Record's label and text fields), placeholder in plain words ("Search diet concerns…", from the Record Type label). Expression filtering moves behind an "Advanced filter" control that uses the F4 condition builder. View mode words from `glossary.ts`.
- **F3. Field settings that fit and make sense.** The field settings dialog becomes a side sheet (drawer on mobile) that never exceeds the viewport and scrolls inside. Sections: Basics (label, type, required, default, help text) open; "Rules for this field" (required when, read only when, show when, must be true, formula) and "Advanced" (key, pattern, permission level, unique, indexed, set once, do not copy, allow on submit) collapsed. Plain-word labels and one-line help with no `doc.` syntax shown; conditions use the F4 builder. Anything that refers to existing items is a select, never free text: field keys, link target Record Type, child Record Type, options source, permission level (named levels). Totals of numeric child fields offered as picks.
- **F4. Rules and lists without code.** A condition builder: "When [record] [field ▾] [is / is not / is empty / is any of / more than …] [value or field ▾]", AND/OR groups, field and Record names autocomplete as you type, it compiles to the existing expression language (`internal/expr`) and round-trips; a "Show as expression" toggle for advanced users. Uses it in: Rules page (validation, guard, derivation for common cases without Starlark; Starlark stays for advanced), field conditions (F3) and filters (F2). **Lists** (saved queries) such as "RSVP yes but missing diet or parking details": shows the live list, exports to PDF now, and has "Email them" and "Message them" buttons that queue through the C2 outbox (delivery provider pending, so the button says the message is queued). An audience rule: "Invited but not responded" are excluded from the day-of schedule send. Research first (a read-only Codex thinker is fine): Airtable and Notion filter builders, HubSpot list and workflow builders; write the design into `.agents/tasks/beta-f4/design.md` and an ADR (Proposed) before code.
- **F5. Circle Space sample Workspace.** `athena new "Circle Space" --sample event-management` (and a Setup Wizard choice later in D5) creates a full test company managed only through Athena. Pack "Event Management" with Modules Events, Guests and RSVP, Catering, Vendors and Rentals, Inventory, Billing. Record Types (names may be refined): Client, Venue, Event (schedule items as child lines), Guest, Invitation, RSVP (response, plus-ones, dietary needs, allergies note, needs parking, vehicle registration, consent), Dietary Requirement, Supplier, Caterer, Dish (recipe lines: ingredient, quantity per serving, unit), Ingredient (perishable, shelf life), Menu (dishes and servings per event), Purchase Order, Goods Receipt, Stock Issue to Event, Rental Item (chairs, tables, tents, sound), Rental Booking, Vendor (DJ, MC, photographer), Vendor Booking (role, fee, status), Seating Table and Seat Assignment, Parking Pass, Sales Invoice, Purchase Invoice, Payment (M-Pesa, bank, cash). Ledger masters for Kenya per Z1. Seed realistic Kenyan data: two events (a corporate launch and a wedding, Nairobi venues), about 120 guests with mixed RSVP states and dietary needs, caterers, DJs, MCs, rentals, stock, invoices and payments. Reports: guest list by RSVP status, dietary summary for the caterer, ingredient needs (servings × recipes) against stock with a shortfall purchase list, stock movements, event profit and loss, unpaid invoices. Rules from F4 prefilled (missing diet or parking details, not responded, headcount over venue capacity, allergen clash between a dish and a guest). Public pages (D3): a per-invitation RSVP page reached by a signed link, and an event page whose schedule shows only to guests who said yes.
