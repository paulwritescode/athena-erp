# Athena ERP • Starter catalogue and business movements

Proposed product contract · 20 November 2026 · [Product playbook](Athena-ERP-Playbook.md) · [Research and audit](Research-and-audit.md)

## What appears after a clean install

Create the Workspace and its database first. The setup wizard offers **Business starter** as the recommended choice and **Empty Workspace** for builders. Business starter installs first-party Packs into that database. Opening the desk after setup shows Customers, Suppliers, Items, Purchases, Sales, Stock, Expenses, Finance and Reports according to the signed-in user’s Grants. Customers and Suppliers are filtered views of Party and its profiles. A customer who later becomes a supplier keeps one Party identity. Separate receivable and payable balances and account mappings remain visible.

Code/data boundary for a seed rerun: use a stable (Pack, seed key, scope) identity. Store the last installed hash and version separately from user-customised values. Insert missing rows, update managed values when unchanged locally, and present a three-way diff on collision. Never recreate a retired account as a new active one because its label changed. Record checkpoints for schema publication and data seeds, fail closed on incompatible dependencies, and verify the expected manifest at resume. Demo transactions belong in a separate explicitly selected demo Workspace.

## Record Type dictionary

These are proposed logical definitions and baseline fields. The physical schema follows the repository naming rules. Existing identifiers take precedence wherever defined. “Setup” means a company-specific row must be entered or approved. “Reference” means a Pack may own versioned rows. “Empty” means the definition is installed and ready for business data. “Generated” means only the indicated kernel operation may create the row. Practical defaults derive from the cross-product process comparison, not a measured ranking of industry usage.

|---|---|---|
| Company, Branch, Department, Cost Centre | Master · setup | Company code, legal identity, base currency, timezone. Branch and cost centre belong to company. Prevent hierarchy cycles and posting to group nodes. Archive referenced nodes and keep historical paths reproducible. |
| Currency, Exchange Rate | Master · reference / setup | Currency code, exponent and rounding policy. Rate keyed by pair, rate type, source and effective instant. Store direction and precision. Reject ambiguous matches. Freeze rate and both amounts on the voucher. |
| Fiscal Year, Accounting Period | Master · setup | Company, book, local date boundaries, close status, close actor. No overlapping periods within a calendar. Soft close restricts normal posting. Hard close requires a separately approved reopen or current-period correction. |
| Account, Account Type, Account Determination | Master · setup / reference | Account code, tree parent, group/leaf, class, currency, control-account role. Mapping resolves company, transaction, item/party group, warehouse or tax category to an active leaf. Missing or equally specific matches block preview and submit. |
| Dimension Definition, Budget | Meta / Master · setup | Define source Type, permitted members, mandatory accounts, defaults, validity and combinations. Budget lines carry scenario, revision, period, account and dimensions. Actual, commitment and release have distinct event identities. |

### Fields and physical relationships that buy future compatibility

Use UUIDv7 for the internal ID under the repository’s storage design. Keep business number, supplier reference and idempotency key distinct. A translated label or changed print layout cannot change identity. Physical patterns are `athena_<name>` for Core state, `p_<pack>__<record_type>` for a Pack Type, `p_<pack>__<record_type>__<child_field>` for its owned children and `l_<ledger>` for shared Ledger tables. Before Pack installation exists, workspace-authored Types use `p_workspace__<key>`. Do not hardwire domain Types into that reserved namespace.

| Field family | Storage and validation contract | Historical consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Text, code, email, phone, URL, barcode, rich text | Explicit length and normalisation. Keep leading zeroes on codes. Sanitise rendered rich text. Carry locale separately from canonical key. | A label change, translated address or barcode alias cannot relink old vouchers. |
| Money, rate, decimal, percent, quantity | Money uses integer minor units plus currency. Rates and quantities use exact decimal strings across RPC/JS and declared scales in storage. Preserve line and document rounding policy, UoM and exchange snapshot. | Allocate indivisible remainders by stable line ID; overflow or an unrepresentable quantity is a validation error. |
| Date, instant, duration, effective range | Business dates in company calendar; instants in UTC. End-exclusive effective windows and controlled overlaps. Keep due date, value date, posting date and recorded time separately. | A late invoice or holiday change leaves earlier “known at” reports reproducible. |
| Link, dynamic link, child table, tree parent, dimensions | Validate Type, scope, stable ID and existence. Dynamic links use a permitted Type list. Child ID survives reorder. Prevent cycles and cross-company links unless explicitly permitted. | Two lines with identical item and price retain different origins; use IDs for allocation. |
| Enum, boolean, JSON, attachment, signature | Stable enum keys, schema-versioned JSON for peripheral attributes, typed columns for joins and constraints. Attachment metadata stores owner, digest, media type, size and immutable version. | Keep the signed document digest and signer evidence. A later upload cannot silently replace the issued copy. |
| Formula, rollup, lookup | Declare dependencies and evaluation order, reject cycles. Freeze tax, price and address at defined lifecycle event. Keep live balances derived from movements. | Repricing an Item never reprices a submitted invoice. Sensitive inputs cannot leak through computed columns. |

### Three concrete relational contracts

**Sales Invoice.** One company and customer profile own many invoices. Each invoice has one or more Invoice Item rows and zero or more Tax Lines and instalments. Header fields include ID, business number, revision, company, customer, posting/due dates, currency, base currency, exchange snapshot, net, tax and gross. A line carries its own stable ID, item or service, exact quantity/unit price, net amount, account and dimensions. A service line may omit warehouse. For a shipped stock line require an application to delivery or an explicit combined-shipment mode. Check header gross equals sum of net and tax including declared rounding. A source allocation prevents combining an already posted delivery with another stock issue.

**Document flow.** One order line can feed many receipts or deliveries. One invoice line can consolidate several source lines. Persist an application with source Type/Record/line, target Type/Record/line, purpose, base units, amount, currency and reversal reference. Index both endpoints and lock source consumption during submit. Maintain *ordered → delivered*, *delivered → billed*, *returned*, and *settled* separately. “Closed short” needs an authorised residual and reason. Cancelling billing releases billed quantity only. A customer credit for price alone never restocks the item.

**Posting and open items.** A submitted voucher revision owns one posting group per declared event/book. Enforce uniqueness under the database transaction. Each Ledger line references that group and its source line. A payment can settle multiple instalments; a single instalment can take partial payments. Keep an append-only settlement application with both endpoints and reversal links. Multi-currency settlement retains document, payment and base amounts. Lock both residuals while allocating. With a party filter, compare the signed residuals to that company’s control account at the identical cutoff. A manual journal to a control account must create the corresponding party item or be refused.

## Movement coverage by business cycle

For each process family, the [Process and event catalogue](Athena-Process-and-Event-Catalogue.md) maps human, device, provider and scheduled triggers to authoritative movements, automatic follow-up and approval gates.

GL denotes the general ledger. GRNI is goods received not invoiced. WIP is work in progress. Example journal directions assume accrual accounting and perpetual stock. Account determination, company policy, local tax treatment and reporting book decide the actual accounts.

| Cycle | Documents and operational movement | Financial movement and required exception |
|---|---|---|
| Lead to order | Lead → Opportunity → Quotation revision → Sales Order. Approve price deviation, check credit exposure and optionally reserve available stock. | Quoting has no GL. An order changes commitments and availability. Expiry, rejection or short close releases residual reservations. |
| Ship, bill and collect | Pick and lot allocation → Delivery Note → Sales Invoice → Payment Entry → Settlement. Allow partial and consolidated billing. | Ship Dr COGS / Cr Stock. Invoice Dr Receivable / Cr Revenue and output tax. Receipt Dr Bank / Cr Receivable. Combined invoice posts stock only if it owns dispatch. |
| Return, credit, refund | Inspect original delivery and invoice. Accept return into quarantine, release or scrap on disposition. Issue price-only credit independently. | Stock return reverses issue cost into the appropriate location. Credit reverses billed value/tax under local policy. Refund extinguishes outstanding customer credit. |
| Advances, deferred revenue, collections | Receive deposit, apply over multiple invoices, recognise service over schedule, issue dunning and approve write-off. | Record liability until applied; keep application distinct from cash receipt. Recognition Dr Deferred Revenue / Cr Revenue. Write-off clears identified receivable and records reason. |
| Request to pay | Requisition → RFQ → Supplier Quotation → PO → Purchase Receipt or service acceptance → Purchase Invoice → release → payment. | PO commits budget. Receipt Dr Stock / Cr GRNI. Invoice clears matched GRNI, books input tax/price variance and AP. Pay Dr AP / Cr Bank; keep under/overbill exception at line grain. |
| Return to supplier, freight, consignment | Link return to receipt and invoice; track supplier credit separately. Allocate freight by weight, quantity or value. Track supplier-owned goods by custody. | Return physical quantity once. Reverse/adjust payable only with appropriate financial document. Allocate freight between stock and consumed cost. Consigned custody alone need not recognise owned inventory. |
| Stock, transit and counting | Reserve, pick, dispatch to transit, partially receive, investigate lost units. Count blind at cutoff, record later movements, approve variance. | Internal transfer conserves quantity/value within owner. Transit loss or write-off posts approved expense. Count variance posts delta; concurrent sale cannot be overwritten by an absolute count. |
| Lot, quality, recall, repack | Lot/serial receipt, inspection, quarantine, release, expiry, split/merge, returns and genealogy. Track quantity by disposition and ownership. | Quarantine can affect availability without changing company stock value. Recall traverses receipt, transformations, customers and remaining bins. Scrap changes stock and expense once. |
| Plan and manufacture | BOM version, routing, workstation, MRP proposals, Work Order, issue to WIP, Job Card, output, by-products, rework and scrap. | WIP accumulates inputs/labour/overhead. Allocate cost to finished goods, by-products, residual WIP and variance. Partial output keeps unfinished value; substitute components still trace to input lots. |
| Subcontract and drop-ship | Send owned components to external custody; receive processed item plus service bill. For direct delivery link supplier fulfilment to customer order. | Custody transfer retains ownership. Add processing service to output cost once. A direct shipment needs evidence of transfer and a cost source without inventing a warehouse receipt. |
| Expense and employee advance | Advance → receipted claim → approval → offset → reimbursement/refund. Rate schedule covers mileage and per diem. Match card statement separately. | Advance Dr Employee Advance / Cr Bank. Claim books expense and recoverable input tax under policy. Offset advance; pay residual only. A card charge clears card liability separately. |
| Asset life | Purchase or accumulate CWIP, capitalise, split components, assign custodian, depreciate by book, maintain, impair, revalue, transfer and dispose. | Acquisition, depreciation, impairment and disposal post under the selected book. Custody transfer changes history only unless a financial transfer is needed. Disposal derecognises cost and accumulated depreciation and records proceeds/gain/loss. |
| Treasury | Bank statement import, split/many-to-many match, fees, internal transfers, bounced cheque, cash count, FX remeasurement, loan schedules. | Statement import alone posts nothing. Matched payments remain single GL movements. Missing charges create approved vouchers. FX distinguishes realised settlement from book remeasurement. |
| Projects and service | Project, Task, Timesheet, parts, milestones, field visit, SLA, warranty. Assign time/cost to project and task; bill once per approved allocation. | Labour/material/expense accumulate by project. Separate billable, billed, recognised and collected. A fixed-fee contract can require WIP/deferred revenue and approved recognition policy. |
| People and payroll | Effective assignments, shifts, attendance, leave awards/use/expiry, salary components, payroll run, slips, remittance and final settlement. | Leave is a unit Ledger. Payroll balances earnings, employer charges, deductions, net-pay and statutory liabilities; payment clears them. Retro corrections retain original period and payslip relationship. Restrict individual pay details. |
| Recurring and usage billing | Contract, rate plan version, Meter Run, late readings, minimum commitment and service period. Schedule invoices by unique contract/period. | Bill usage once and adjust late readings by delta. Invoice date, service period and revenue recognition may differ. Cancellation of renewal does not remove past invoices. |
| Retail/POS | Shift open, float, sale, split tender, refund, cash count, shift close and deposit. Retain device and terminal receipt identity. | Sales, tax, COGS, cash/card clearing and drawer variance reconcile to shift. Authorisation, capture, settlement, fees and chargeback have separate provider references. |
| Budgeting, tax and close | Approve budget revision, consume/release commitments, accrue/reverse, file tax, lock book/period and snapshot. | Release commitments as actuals arrive to avoid double counting. Compare tax base and tax movements to GL. Lock only after reconciliations and in-flight postings/revaluations finish. |
| Intercompany and consolidation | Paired documents with shared pair ID and counterparty, due-to/from, translation by book, elimination and group close. | Separate legal books remain balanced. ## Worked acceptance records

### Purchase, sell, return and close

Single company, one currency, zero tax, one FIFO layer, perpetual stock, no opening balances. The amounts below are test fixtures. A buyer orders 10 units at 100. A storekeeper receives 6. Accounts payable receives an invoice for 8 at 100 against that same order. Under the proposed strict receipt-based policy the invoice stays on hold. The clerk obtains a corrected invoice for 6. The remaining 4 ordered units are open.

| Committed event | Debit | Credit | Operational evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Receive 6 | Stock 600 | GRNI 600 | Layer 6 @ 100. Open receipt accrual 600. |
| Bill 6 | GRNI 600 | Payable 600 | Allocate invoice to receipt line. Open GRNI zero. |
| Deliver 4 | COGS 400 | Stock 400 | Four units applied to the original receipt. Two remain. |
| Invoice 4 @ 150 | Receivable 600 | Revenue 600 | Customer open item 600. No second stock issue. |
| Collect 450 | Bank 450 | Receivable 450 | Settlement 450. Customer owes 150. |
| Return one saleable unit | Stock 100 | COGS 100 | Return references original delivery line and layer. Three units remain. |
| Credit customer 150 | Revenue 150 | Receivable 150 | Credit applied to residual invoice. Customer owes zero. |
| Pay supplier 300 | Payable 300 | Bank 300 | Supplier open item 300. |

Expected closing balances are Stock 300 debit, Bank 150 debit, COGS 300 debit, Payable 300 credit and Revenue 450 credit. Trial balance totals are 750 per side. Net income is 150. Assets 450 equal liabilities 300 plus current earnings 150. Open PO quantity is 4. Stock 3 at 100 equals its control account. A report dated before the credit still shows the receivable outstanding if the credit has a later posting date.

**Late freight variant.** A separate approved freight invoice for 60 attributable to these six received units arrives after the return. In this fixture allocate 10 per unit. Three remain and three have been consumed net. Dr Stock 30, Dr COGS 30, Cr Payable 60. The stock quantity stays 3 and its value becomes 330. Gross profit becomes 120. A sealed earlier Report Run still shows 300 stock. If the original period is closed, post the adjustment in the next permissible period and record the original attribution date. Any statutory restatement is a controlled book/period decision.

## Designing the second implementation without breaking the first

### What a Pack author must declare

Under the supplied Pack layout, a trading Pack has a `pack.yaml`, a Module manifest, `record_types/`, `rules/`, `queries/`, `reports/`, `workflows/`, plus Pack-level roles, seed, migrations, locales and test fixtures. Cross-Pack identifiers follow `pack:record_type`, including the supplied examples `accounts:account` and `stock:item`. Pin the owner of each shared definition before shipping the first release. The kernel catalogue groups Item under catalogue, while its worked qualified key is `stock:item`. Preserve that key and let catalogue/selling Modules expose the same definition. Installing a sales-only service business may require Item metadata without enabling physical inventory workflows. A capability dependency and a separate activation setting solve that distinction.

| Contract to freeze first | Example decision | Verification fixture |
|---|---|---|
| Grain of a stock movement | One owner, company, item, lot/serial, stock UoM, source line and location leg. A transfer consists of correlated legs. Quantities on a serial cannot be fractional. | Dispatch 5, receive 3, leave 2 in transit. Sum by owner across locations is conserved. |
| Account mapping | Resolve most-specific effective mapping within company/book. If two equal-priority candidates match, identify both and stop. Store resolved account and mapping revision. | Change the default expense account after posting. Preview of a new expense uses the new default. Reversal still hits the old account. |
| Ledger dimensions | Attach typed members to individual lines. Allocate one header cost to several cost centres using stable exact weights. Enforce membership, company and validity on each line. | A purchase spanning branches can be consolidated by company. Drill-down for one branch includes its share only. |
| Price and tax | Record quantity, unit price, discounts, net, tax basis, tax lines, currency, rates and rounding policy. ### Business configuration that belongs outside the universal posting rules

A first-party country Pack should declare its jurisdiction, valid periods, chart and tax mappings, currency/rounding, invoice numbering and required printed fields, statutory report schema versions, payroll deductions where supported and an external filing receipt model. Country-specific rules need dated evidence and a reviewer. An amendment carries the originally filed return, adjustment period, new submission reference and response. Distinguish a calculated return, a reviewed return, a transmitted return and an accepted return. A provider timeout leaves the filing outcome unknown until reconciliation. This edition selects no jurisdiction or statutory rate.

A service business may recognise revenue by milestone while a merchant recognises it on a specified delivery event. A distributor may hold third-party stock at the same warehouse as its own. A group may run management and statutory books with different rates or asset lives. Model the recognition trigger, ownership and book on the transaction. An editable global toggle that reinterprets historical Records would make prior statements irreproducible.

### Four reconciliation equations for reviewers

1. **Customer instalment at cutoff** = invoiced amount − effective credits − effective receipt/advance applications + linked reversals, all in that instalment’s currency. Show unapplied cash elsewhere. Sum converted open items per control account using the book’s FX policy.
2. **Receipt exposure** = accepted quantity − quantities applied to bills − unbilled returns, adjusted for explicit application reversals. GRNI value is the sum of the corresponding clearing postings. A quantity variance and a price variance can have different signs.
3. **Owned stock at cutoff** = opening units + external receipts + accepted customer returns − external issues − supplier returns ± approved count adjustments. Internal transfers cancel only when both legs are in the reporting scope. Cost-only postings change value without changing the quantity equation.
4. **Book roll-forward** = opening carrying amount + capitalisations + qualifying improvements ± revaluations − depreciation − impairment − carrying amount disposed. Evaluate independently for each book and company. Tie the GL only to that book’s posting-enabled schedules.

Each discrepancy row should carry the expected value, observed value, variance, first differing source line, cutoff and assigned owner. Resolution is a linked correction or a documented policy exception. Acknowledging an alert never changes a financial balance.
