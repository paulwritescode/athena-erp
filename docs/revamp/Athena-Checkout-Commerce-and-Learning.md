## 1. What actually makes a cashier’s shift difficult

In ECR Retail Loss’s survey of self-checkout supervisors, age checks, security tags and weight alerts stood out as frequent interruptions. Staff also reported unreadable barcodes, difficulties finding loose items and payment trouble. Sixty-three percent said they could not cope with their allocation of machines or could cope only when it was quiet. This is evidence from self-checkout staff; use local observation to establish frequencies at staffed Kenyan lanes. OSHA’s grocery guidance adds an easily overlooked cost: repeated twisting, reaching and handling heavy goods at checkout. [ECR frontline research](https://ecrloss.com/research-paper/better-self-checkout-management/) · [OSHA grocery ergonomics](https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3192.pdf)

The following priority reflects harm, queue disruption and recoverability. It is a proposed build order. A high-frequency produce lookup and a rarer duplicate charge deserve different responses.

| Priority and cashier’s problem | Athena’s response at the lane | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|
| **First: “The customer says they paid. My screen is still waiting.”** | Show confirmed tenders, outstanding balance, merchant, masked reference, last provider check and **Check payment**. Let the cashier park the case with an owner and keep serving. A fresh debit requires a distinct confirmed action. | Confirmed duplicate debits per 1,000 tenders; unknown attempts by age; customer waiting time; how many cases required finance next day. A zero-duplicate fixture is a release gate. |
| **First: “It won’t scan” or “I scanned it twice.”** | Give immediate accepted/failed feedback and highlight the last line. Offer typed code, name/PLU lookup with produce pictures, quantity correction and price-check request. Support a handheld scanner for bulky items in the trolley. | First-pass scan rate, lookup time, supervisor calls for ordinary corrections, observer count of awkward lifts. Distinguish unknown Item from broken device. |
| **First: “The shelf says a different price.”** | Show the price source, unit, pack size, loyalty condition, offer dates and overlapping discount rules. Let a lead approve a scoped adjustment. Open a shelf-label correction for merchandising from the same screen. | Disputes by SKU/outlet and recurrence after correction; time spent explaining; variance between receipt and approved adjustment. [CMA grocery unit-pricing research](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/64b80ab1ef5371000d7aeefa/CMA_Review_of_unit_pricing_in_the_groceries_sector.pdf) |
| **First: “I need a supervisor and the whole queue has stopped.”** | Open an assistance ticket linked to lane and action, notify an available lead, show acknowledged/approaching status and allow appropriate basket suspension. Physical ID/tag checks remain local. | Alert wait and service time separately, by hour and reason; how many lanes one attendant is covering; abandoned baskets. [ECR frontline research](https://ecrloss.com/research-paper/better-self-checkout-management/) |
| **Next: “The scale, reader, network or printer has frozen.”** | Distinguish the failed component from the sale. Save acknowledged lines, provide a qualified alternative peripheral, support authorised transfer and reprint the same receipt with a copy marker. | Recoveries without rekeying goods, lost baskets, duplicate captures, rejected scale readings, lost or duplicated receipts. |
| **Next: “I’m blamed when the drawer is short.”** | Calculate change in exact currency units, record floats/drops/payouts and split tenders, require handover counts and independent variance review. Blind count precedes disclosure of expected cash. | Over/short by shift and cause, unresolved external tenders, rework at close. Preserve shared-drawer custody changes before attributing a difference. |
| **Next: “The shopper is upset, and I’m on my own.”** | Give the cashier plain-language status they can show the shopper, discreet staff assistance with an accountable responder, and a neutral route to a staffed checkout. Use an incident process with restricted notes. | Time to help, team workload, staff-reported confidence and safety, repeated equipment faults. Management owns staffing, breaks, training and safe physical layout. [Usdaw Freedom From Fear](https://www.usdaw.org.uk/campaigns/freedom-from-fear/) |
| **Across all lanes: fatigue, handover and unfamiliar workflows** | Stable keyboard shortcuts, large touch targets, scanner focus restoration and practice mode using synthetic payments. Cashier change locks the drawer and resumes a recoverable sale with named ownership. | Observe seated/standing use, one-handed scanning, error recovery by a new starter and handover during a pending tender. Never use raw scans/minute as an individual performance ranking. |

**Pilot method.** At a busy and a quieter store, observe opening, peak, shift change and closing with operator consent. Time the scan, payment wait, supervisor wait and reconciliation separately. Classify interruptions by root cause and record what the cashier actually did. Review anonymised exceptions with a cashier, attendant, lead and finance clerk. Compare baseline and candidate on matched basket size, tender mix, staffing and network condition. Publish median and 95th-percentile recovery, denominator, duplicate/lost payment count, queue abandonment and short operator interviews. A faster queue that leaves more customers incorrectly charged fails the trial.

## 2. A supermarket lane that survives real traffic

**Staffed checkout.** Open shift and prove drawer custody → check scanner/scale/printer/reader → scan or weigh products → explain discounts, deposits or restrictions → take one or several tenders → issue receipt → reconcile shift. Support hold/resume, customer display, tax-exempt sale where approved, coupons, loyalty with an anonymous alternative, short cash, cashback only under a qualified policy, sale by pack versus each, delivery/pickup handoff, original-receipt returns and post-close refunds. Each branch selects its supported combinations. A cashier must be able to finish a routine basket without using a mouse or opening a settings screen.

| Device path | What the adapter records | Important distinction and fallback |
|---|---|---|
| USB/Bluetooth keyboard-wedge scanner | Configured terminator, symbology if supplied, raw token held briefly for parsing, register association and decode outcome | Explicit scan input protects phone-number and amount fields from scanner keystrokes. A keyboard wedge alone may not identify the device or expose its health. Provide a test label and manual lookup. |
| Managed countertop imager or handheld | Device ID, driver/bridge version, supported UPC/EAN and approved 2D formats, per-read event ID if available, connect/disconnect and acknowledgement | De-duplicate retransmission by transport/event identity. Two intentional scans of identical cans must add two units. A dual-marked pack can expose two symbols for one physical pass; qualify scanner settings with sample packaging. [Zebra scanner SDK](https://techdocs.zebra.com/dcs/scanners/sdk-windows/) · [GS1 retail 2D guidance](https://ref.gs1.org/guidelines/2d-in-retail/) |
| Integrated scale, deli label or loose produce | Scale reads gross/tare/net, unit, stable flag, instrument and verification state; label decoder identifies item, weight or already-extended price using the retailer’s scheme | Check checksum and recognised prefix/format. Keep leading zeroes. Keep sell-unit, unit price and extended price distinct. Unknown PLU opens catalog lookup; unstable scale prompts reweigh. Test calibration and legal-for-trade requirements locally. [Zebra OPOS scanner and scale drivers](https://techdocs.zebra.com/dcs/scanners/opos-windows/) |
| Card reader, pin pad, printer and cash drawer | Provider merchant/terminal ID, peripheral station binding, capability and last status; print job ID independent of tender | Pair reader and lane deliberately, test the amount on the customer-facing screen, and confirm reader availability before starting. Queue reprints; record authorised no-sale drawer opens. Payment credentials stay in the terminal/provider. [Square Terminal API](https://developer.squareup.com/docs/terminal-api/overview) |

**Burst handling.** At scan time, resolve an exact barcode against a versioned, scoped item index. Show an acknowledgement only after the basket has accepted the line under the active online/offline policy. Keep queued versus committed scans distinguishable. Serialise basket revisions through one writer. Apply visual, audible and screen-reader feedback without requiring a blocking modal for each unit. If the server is unavailable, keep recoverable drafts where the profile permits; price version, saleability, stock and tender eligibility are checked before final acceptance. Test rapid scans, a hot-unplug mid-basket, two lanes sharing scarce stock, and restart between payment and print. Benchmark using actual lane hardware, a store-sized catalog and peak traffic.

### Fixed kiosks and scan-as-you-shop

Walmart’s US mobile flow lets shoppers scan items as they shop, review the basket, scan a self-checkout QR and select wallet payment. Tesco’s UK model also offers retailer handheld scanners linked to Clubcard. A fixed kiosk is a different journey: the shopper scans at the lane and an attendant covers several machines. These examples establish viable hardware and handoff patterns. Membership requirements, audit policies and supported tenders are retailer decisions. [Walmart Mobile Scan & Go](https://www.walmart.com/help/article/walmart-benefits-mobile-scan-and-go/80c77b6a9d6042589ce4e000f10e0447) · [Tesco Scan as you Shop](https://www.tesco.com/help/pages/in-store-faqs/our-facilities-in-store/scan-as-you-shop) · [ECR self-checkout management](https://ecrloss.com/research-paper/better-self-checkout-management/)

| Stage | Shopper’s screen | Attendant and server contract |
|---|---|---|
| Begin | Choose language, accessibility options and Start. Offer guest basket on a fixed kiosk. | Create short-lived, outlet-bound session. Never show the last shopper’s phone, receipt, items or loyalty balance. Disable unavailable payment methods before scanning begins. |
| Scan and bag | Show product, image where useful, quantity, price, discount and running total. Offer produce lookup, own-bag handling, undo and persistent **Help**. | Optional bagging scale compares measured change to configured tolerance. Common false alerts, personal bags and mobility aids require a respectful local resolution. Avoid labelling a shopper as dishonest from a mismatch. |
| Intervention | Explain “A colleague is coming to check this item”. Keep basket and queued items safe. | Attendant sees lane, reason, queue age, accessibility needs voluntarily provided and allowed actions. Claim and resolve ticket with reason. Age verification and physical checks need an authorised person under store policy. |
| Transfer / review | For mobile baskets, display one-use short-lived handoff QR or staff-assisted code. Show the imported basket and confirmation on the assigned lane. | Authenticate register and same outlet. Atomically consume token and transfer basket revision. A copied QR cannot produce a second sale. A permitted rescan has a defined sample/full-check process and appeal to staff. |
| Pay and leave | Select eligible tender; enter mobile number privately if required; show final status, receipt and help. | Issue exit credential only after accepted sale/fiscal policy. Verify single-use credentials under store policy, handle lost receipt, and provide staffed exit on reader failure. A pending payment remains owned by staff. Clear kiosk on completion or safe handoff. |

**Installation boundaries.** A cashless kiosk, a kiosk with cash recycler and a shopper-phone programme require separate qualification. A recycler needs denomination counts, rejected-note handling, change availability, jam recovery, staff cash pickup and daily physical reconciliation. A store may launch staffed tills before enabling any kiosk. Accessibility acceptance includes low reach, large text, high contrast, keyboard/switch operation, audio with a private alternative, non-colour status, staffed help and a way to continue without owning a smartphone. [W3C WCAG 2.2 guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/)

## 3. Payments when the customer, device and server disagree

A payment has at least four clocks: the shopper’s action, provider acknowledgement, provider result, and merchant settlement. A request accepted by an API can still end in a decline. A successful card or wallet payment may reach the merchant bank account later, net of fees. Display each fact in its own field. Keep the cashier’s till responsive while a worker reconciles external status.

| Rail | Initiation and identifiers | How to check what happened |
|---|---|---|
| **Kenya M-PESA Express (STK Push)** | Confirm merchant, amount and authorised destination number; create durable attempt before requesting a prompt. Persist local attempt ID and returned MerchantRequestID/CheckoutRequestID as applicable. Normalise MSISDN using the Kenya adapter, allow correction before send, and show a masked confirmation. Customer enters PIN only on their own provider prompt. | Receive asynchronous result and reconcile using the original CheckoutRequestID and merchant binding. Safaricom’s published SDK demonstrates a dedicated STK query keyed on CheckoutRequestID. Distinguish query transport/acceptance from final transaction result. Result metadata/receipt availability may differ between callback, query and statements. Verify the current Daraja product contract and active merchant entitlement before enabling. [Safaricom Daraja](https://developer.safaricom.co.ke/apis) · [Safaricom SDK query implementation](https://github.com/safaricom/mpesa-php-sdk/blob/master/src/Mpesa.php) |
| **Customer-initiated till or PayBill (C2B)** | Shopper pays the registered merchant independently. Where a PayBill account reference is available, provide a short basket reference and exact amount. Buy Goods Till and PayBill have different customer and merchant flows. Show the merchant identity clearly. | Match trusted confirmations/merchant records to merchant, reference, amount, currency and time. Use provider transaction/receipt ID for the entitled transaction-status or merchant reconciliation route. STK query applies only to an STK request. Phone/time/amount can narrow candidates; a screenshot, SMS or verbal code alone cannot close the sale. [Safaricom PayBill guide](https://www.safaricom.co.ke/images/Downloads/Mpesa-paybill-guide.pdf) · [Daraja transaction status](https://developer.safaricom.co.ke/apis/TransactionStatus) · [M-PESA Business Till](https://business.safaricom.co.ke/products/m-pesa-business-till) |
| **Card-present terminal** | Create attempt, lock device, bind request to lane, merchant, amount/currency and provider request/reference. Send through qualified terminal/API. Track whether the device is awaiting card, PIN, provider response, authorisation, capture or cancellation. | Query the original checkout using the identifiers required by that provider. Adyen documents distinct in-progress, completed and not-found responses and the possibility of a reference mismatch. Square exposes terminal events. A local cancel button cannot guarantee the processor cancelled a payment. [Adyen transaction status](https://docs.adyen.com/point-of-sale/basic-tapi-integration/verify-transaction-status) · [Square terminal webhooks](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/terminal-api/webhooks) |

**When the number is requested.** Offer **Enter on customer display** and **Cashier enters with permission**. Display “Send a KES 350 M-PESA request to •••• 1234?” with **Edit number** and **Send request**. Let a shopper paying from another person’s phone see the amount and confirm that arrangement. A different paying identity does not automatically change the order owner. For an already initiated STK attempt the cashier can recheck without asking for the number again. For an unmatched manual payment, lead with the receipt reference and request masked lookup details only if needed and authorised. Keep contact use specific to the transaction; a mobile-money number never enrols someone in loyalty or messaging. Provide an alternate tender if the shopper declines to share their number.

### Cashier dialog contract

| Trigger / exact headline | What the cashier and customer see | Allowed actions and server behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting for customer approval** | Due KES 350, masked destination or terminal label, request time, neutral instruction, last confirmed step | **Check payment**, **Keep waiting**, **Park sale**. One poll worker per attempt with rate limits, cooldown and backoff. Background polling continues when the modal closes. |
| **Payment status unknown** | “We haven’t confirmed this payment. If you already approved it, please wait while we check.” Show last check time and case ID. Never use a countdown as proof of failure. | **Check payment** queries original attempt; **Call lead**, **Park sale**. Disable fresh prompt/debit for the unresolved amount by default. If a rail has no reliable lookup, route to merchant reconciliation. |
| **Find a payment already made** | Merchant/outlet, due, private optional receipt entry and masked results. A cashier sees only relevant matches. | Exact confirmed match and unused receipt may be applied under policy. Two plausible matches or amount mismatch opens supervisor queue. No blanket lookup by customer number across the chain. |
| **Payment confirmed** | Confirmed leg, remaining balance and receipt/status reference where supplied. Use accessible text and a durable journal entry. | Finish only when required tender and sale conditions hold. Duplicate callback or poll result returns the same posting; an older pending observation cannot reverse a success. |
| **Declined, cancelled or expired by provider** | Show customer-safe reason, remaining due and tender alternatives. Separate provider-confirmed finality from a local timeout. | Permit another method or a **new** attempt with explicit amount and customer confirmation when finality is established. Store a predecessor link. |
| **Different amount / received twice** | Confirmed sums, outstanding or excess, source and case ID. Differentiate unapplied customer money from income. | Post only eligible allocation. Supervisor reconciles excess to unapplied funds/refund per policy. A later reversal/chargeback is a new business event. |
| **Suspended sale ready for follow-up** | Cashier sees next owner, basket locator and handoff receipt clearly marked **Payment pending**. Customer gets an optional privacy-safe reference. | Open a new basket without releasing the goods as paid. Another lane can resume under a new writer lease. Finance sees unsettled cases at close. |

**Payment records.** A Tender Attempt is one request against one tender leg. Store local operation key, basket/sale revision, merchant binding, environment, lane/terminal, amount/currency, provider IDs, masked customer reference, provider/result class, last check, next allowed check, confirmed amount, timestamps and linked exception. A Payment Observation (Log) stores callback/query/statement source, received time, provider event ID or deduplication fingerprint, authenticity verification result, payload digest and restricted evidence. A Payment Allocation links confirmed money to sale/leg. An Unmatched Payment Case names owner, reason, candidate references, resolution and review. A Settlement Batch contains actual credit, fees, reversals, cutoff and bank reference. Preserve provider receipt uniqueness per rail, merchant and environment, and allow explicit audited split allocations up to the confirmed total.

**Concurrency and integrity.** Verify callback origin/authenticity by each provider’s documented mechanism; where callback assurance is insufficient, verify through an authenticated status/statement path before accepting the money. Persist inbox and advance attempt/allocations transactionally; send UI updates after commit. Use a unique provider transaction identity, payload digest and amount/currency/merchant validation. If identifiers conflict or a success omits required evidence, quarantine and reconcile. If the connection fails *before an STK checkout ID is returned*, a phone number is not a substitute query key. Keep “initiation uncertain”, search eligible merchant evidence, and leave the request unresolved until policy permits disposition. One original charge must stay protected from operator double-clicks, callback-before-response races, simultaneous manual and automated checks and retries after process restart.

**Worked lunch-hour incident.** Basket KES 1,350. Cashier accepts KES 1,000 cash, sends one STK request for 350, then the network drops. **Check payment** runs twice. A success callback arrives while a query is running. Final sale has one KES 1,000 cash allocation and one KES 350 wallet allocation, remaining due zero, one invoice and one stock issue. If the customer also made an independent KES 350 PayBill transfer, record that additional confirmed money once as unapplied until resolved. Till sales stay 1,350. If settlement deducts 5 on the original wallet leg, reconcile 345 bank plus 5 fee to 350 wallet clearing. Fees and amounts are fixtures. Test pending at midnight, lost callback, key rotation, wrong merchant, identical amounts from different shoppers, refund after settlement and a terminal restart before receipt.

## 4. One product catalogue, several sales channels

Publish a **projection** of the catalogue. The merchant picks sellable Items/variants, descriptions, approved images, prices, channels and fulfilment locations. A Go service composes channel-specific listings and keeps revision and sync status. A public storefront, Instagram catalogue or TikTok listing sees only published fields. Direct SQL access would expose costs or drafts, bypass Grants and leave no record of which price a shopper saw.

[Shopify webhook mechanics](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/webhooks) · [Shopify inventory integrations](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/inventory-management-apps) |

**Storefront.** Pack ships an admin product table with publish/draft, validation results and preview; public category/search and product pages with images, variants, unit prices, stock freshness and pickup/delivery terms; cart, guest checkout, consent controls, customer-safe payment recovery, order status, returns and refunds. Reuse a public read API over published views and a checkout command with server-quoted prices. Currency, language and outlet selection travel with the cart. Inventory, customer details and orders must remain outside public edge caches. On final-item contention, one reservation wins; the second shopper sees a changed quantity or alternative before paying. If money arrives after a reservation expires, place the order in review and give the buyer a resolution path.

### Outlet catalogue, collection orders and phone baskets

The [Process and event catalogue](Athena-Process-and-Event-Catalogue.md) follows a prepaid cake from reservation through provider confirmation, staging and single-use handover, including expiry and duplicate delivery.

A shop can publish its assortment through the same outlet-scoped catalogue used for a restaurant menu. A QR at the entrance opens the branch page in the shopper’s browser. Someone at home can open the same page from a link. The first choice is **“Shop for me”** or **“I’ll pick my items”**. The second choice is **when to pay**. Keeping those choices independent supports four journeys with the same Cart, Sales Order, Reservation and Tender Attempt.

| Journey | What the shopper gets | What staff do to finish |
|---|---|---|
| Staff pick · pay at collection | Order ticket showing pickup window, estimate and **Pay at counter** | Pick and stage the order. Scan the ticket and container labels, review the final bill, take the balance and release the goods. |
| Staff pick · prepay | Order ticket, payment receipt after confirmation, then a separate **Ready to collect** notice | Reserve the goods, pack them, and match the ticket to the staged containers. If contents and confirmed payment agree, hand them over with zero due. |
| Shopper picks · pay at counter | Phone basket with branch prices, running estimate and a handoff QR/code | Scan physical goods and retrieve the phone basket, in either order. Resolve differences, take payment for the final basket and issue the sale receipt. |
| Shopper picks · prepay | Confirmation for the paid basket and a QR/code to present at the counter | Independently scan the physical items, compare line by line, verify the merchant’s payment record and resolve any difference before release. |

**What the public page promises.** Show branch name, hours, pickup counter, sellable variants, price and unit (each, pack or kg), promotion conditions, availability and last update. Optional aisle/department hints help people shopping for themselves. Availability is outlet-specific and can change while someone browses. Display **Available**, **Low stock**, **Sold out** or **Availability unknown** according to freshness and policy. On branch change, retain a shopping list if useful and recheck every price, quantity and pickup promise. Keep internal costs, exact bin locations and other customers’ holds private.

**When something is actually reserved.** Adding the last cake to a cart gives an estimate. To promise it, the server must atomically claim available stock for that outlet, variant, quantity and pickup window. Reservations reduce quantity available to promise without creating a sale or stock issue. Set a short, disclosed hold while the payment is in progress; convert it to an order reservation after confirmed payment. A pay-at-counter order can reserve until its agreed collection deadline. A bakery may offer a future production slot only against configured capacity and a promise time. Mark a future cake **Pre-order** so the buyer can distinguish it from one already available. A late payment after a hold expires opens a stock/payment exception; staff can offer an agreed replacement, later collection or refund.

#### How collection works behind the counter

1. **Receive the order.** The customer confirms outlet, items, pickup window, substitution preference and payment choice. Persist one order with stable source lines. Show the ticket immediately, offer **Download/print ticket**, and email a copy if the customer supplies an address for this order. Email failure is a delivery status; the downloadable ticket and the staff order still exist.
2. **Assign and label.** The outlet queue sorts by promised time and storage needs. A picker claims the order. Queue a sticker for each trolley, basket, bag or cake box with outlet, short order code, container ID, sequence such as **1 of 3**, pickup window and handling cue. Omit the customer’s phone, email and payment reference. If the printer stalls, staff can attach a numbered reusable tag and bind it by scan. A reprint keeps the container identity.
3. **Pick and pack.** Against each ordered line, scan the actual variant and record picked quantity, unit or measured weight. Record short picks and proposed substitutions explicitly. The picker sees the customer’s preference and requests approval where required. A higher total needs customer agreement and a verified additional tender. Track which container holds each line. Staging a chilled bag and an ambient basket in separate locations must leave both discoverable under one order.
4. **Stage and notify.** Scan each container into a staging location; mark the order ready only when its promised contents and exceptions are accounted for. Send **Ready to collect** with location, collection deadline and outstanding balance. A prepayment acknowledgement alone cannot trigger this message.
5. **Collect or expire.** At pickup, scan or enter the customer’s code, then scan the staged containers. Show expected versus found, including chilled or bakery items, and require a disposition for missing goods. For an authorised alternate collector or a lost ticket, apply the outlet’s limited verification and reissue policy. On cancellation or no-show, release outstanding reservations, record restock/inspection or perishable waste, and route confirmed money for the applicable refund or credit. A returned cake cannot silently become saleable because a reservation expired.

#### Cashier verification in either scan order

The till can start with the **ticket first** or with **physical goods first**. In the second path, hold the scans as a provisional lane basket. Scanning a ticket, typing an order code or locating an authorised merchant payment reference attaches that basket to the existing order and stops an independent checkout. A reference that resolves to several candidates goes to staff review.

The comparison screen has three columns: **Ordered**, **Actually scanned/picked**, **Payment confirmed**. Compare variants, units, quantities or actual weights, applicable prices/discounts and final tax. Equal totals can hide a different item. For staff-picked orders the picker’s verified line scans plus sealed container checks can satisfy the contents check under outlet policy; a broken seal or discrepancy requires a line rescan. For shopper-picked prepaid orders, use a full physical line scan in the proposed baseline.

| At the counter | Next action |
|---|---|
| Goods match and trusted confirmed allocations cover the final amount | Show **Paid · zero due** and **Complete handover**. Store handover, sale/fulfilment reference and one-time redemption together, and issue stock exactly once under the configured posting owner. |
| Goods match and payment is due | Show **Pay KES …** for the outstanding amount only. Credit any confirmed deposit or split tender. On confirmed payment return to the same handover. |
| Extra, missing, substituted or differently weighed goods | Highlight the affected lines, even with zero price difference. Offer to remove/replace the item or obtain agreement to an order revision. Keep the original ticket and payment history. Collect a positive difference; send an overpayment through the approved refund/credit process and record who owns it. |
| Customer presents an M-PESA SMS, payment ID or downloaded receipt but the merchant has no confirmed match | Show **Payment unconfirmed**. Use **Check payment** on the original STK/card attempt or **Find merchant payment** for a customer-initiated transfer. A lead may apply verified, unallocated merchant evidence. Otherwise park the case and keep the goods staged. Neither recheck sends a fresh charge request. [Payment recovery contract](#payments) |
| Wrong outlet, expired/reissued ticket, already collected, or connection lost during completion | Show the recorded status, authorised lookup and responsible desk. After a timeout retrieve the existing handover by operation ID. Keep final release online in the initial design so two tills cannot release the same order. |

**Documents the shopper can recognise.** The **order ticket** identifies the order, outlet, promised window and how to collect. The **payment receipt** records confirmed money, amount, currency and reference. The **collection confirmation** records which goods were released and when. A single customer page may display all three, each with its own status, date and download. The entrance QR is reusable and public. A customer ticket uses an opaque, order-scoped claim token plus a rate-limited manual fallback code. Scanning or downloading a ticket does not consume it; only a committed handover redeems it. Reissue invalidates earlier claim credentials while preserving receipts and audit history. Public status links disclose limited information and never grant staff actions.

## 5. Bind WhatsApp to Records without exposing the database

A template is an approved message format. A Flow is an interactive sequence of screens. A webhook is an inbound event. An Athena Rule can request a notification through the outbox; only a narrowly authorised Go command can use a validated response to change a Record. Meta defines templates under a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). Flows may be static or use a data-exchange endpoint. Free-form messaging depends on the applicable customer-service window; approved templates, opt-in, purpose and provider policy govern outbound contact. Re-evaluate eligibility at send time, including after a queued retry. [Meta template overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview) · [AWS WhatsApp messaging windows](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/social-messaging/latest/userguide/whatsapp-send-message.html) · [WhatsApp Business policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy)

|---|---|---|
| Channel Binding / Setting | Workspace, WABA, phone-number ID, tenant/business identity, environment, scoped credential refs, webhook subscription and status, supported markets/locales | **Settings → Integrations → WhatsApp**. Connect, test inbound/outbound with approved test recipient, check ownership, rotate or disconnect. |
| Message Template Binding / Meta | Athena stable key, WABA/provider template ID/name/language/category, provider approval/status, variable names/types/order, permitted source paths, redaction and revision | Select a template and map variables from a Record Type using field pickers. | Flow Definition and Binding / Meta | Provider Flow ID/status and schema/data-API version, entry screen, allowed inputs/outputs, static or endpoint-backed mode, endpoint key reference, destination command, target Type, field allowlist | Build mode offers schema validation, sample rendering and test exchange. **Example A — supermarket pickup substitution.** A picker discovers the ordered pack size is unavailable. A Go command creates a substitution proposal with allowed options, price impact and expiry. An opted-in customer gets a permitted message linking a Flow. An endpoint-backed screen reads only the authorised proposal and returns the allowed choices. Completion validates the Flow token, signed/encrypted endpoint protocol as applicable, customer binding, offer version, stock, price and remaining validity. It then accepts one substitution or records rejection. If the source order changed while the Flow was open, show “Selection has changed” and offer human contact. An uplift in price follows explicit payment approval. Session completion never independently debits the original tender. [WhatsApp Flow concepts](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/social-messaging/latest/userguide/managing-flows.html) · [Meta’s Go endpoint example](https://github.com/WhatsApp/WhatsApp-Flows-Tools/blob/main/examples/endpoint/go/flows-endpoint/main.go)

**Example B — learning reminders.** A scheduled assessment reminder resolves student or authorised guardian, class, date, approved locale and opt-in. The template links to the authenticated student portal. The channel message contains the minimum useful detail. Grades, answer keys, identity evidence and full transcripts require authenticated, scoped access. If a guardian relationship expires before delivery, suppress the queued message. On enrolment inquiries, a Flow may create a private admissions draft and invite the learner to complete verification in the portal. A social identity or phone number alone cannot create a verified student identity.

**Tests before enabling.** Exercise opt-out between schedule and send, missing locale, template rejection, expired service window, duplicate completion, replay against another Workspace, endpoint-key rotation, price change while selecting and an unavailable messaging provider. Audit source event → delivery → response → authorised business command → Record revision. A Flow validation error must leave the existing customer order or learner record intact.

Give Teaching, Assessment and Registrar separate Modules. One institution may install Learning with no commerce capability. A fee-charging school can bind Accounts to approved invoices and payments, but access to financial Records must not confer authority to edit grades. A student is a verified learner identity with a scoped membership. A course is a reusable definition; a course offering is its versioned delivery to a class in a particular term. A mark belongs to a specific assessment attempt or approved grade item.

| Learner / Master; Guardian Relationship / effective-dated Master | Student number, verified identity mapping, preferred/display name, institution/programme, status; guardian identity, relationship, authorised scope and validity | No identity merge by phone alone. **End-to-end case.** An instructor publishes notes and a five-question quiz, then assigns a practical with a rubric. A student can read the notes, post a question, save quiz answers twice after a reconnect, submit once and receive a timestamped acknowledgement. A grader marks the practical and a moderator approves it. With a 40% quiz weight and a 60% practical weight, scores of 80/100 and 70/100 yield **74/100**. A reviewed correction of the practical to 75 yields **77/100**. The original issued transcript stays verifiable as superseded; a new issue points to it. No credits accrue twice. Include tests for excused assessment/reweighting, incomplete result, retake substitution, withdrawal after midterm, late submission, lost connection, accommodations, grade appeal and revoked guardian access. GPA and letter grade are produced only when a published institution scheme defines them.

**Interop choices.** Use OneRoster where a school needs compatible roster, enrolment and gradebook exchange. Use QTI for supported assessment item/test import and export; report incompatible interaction types to authors. LTI can launch approved external learning tools and return scoped outcomes when the appropriate services are configured. CLR provides a standard for exchanging learner achievements. A statutory or official transcript still needs institutional content rules, issuer authority and issuance controls. Advertise versioned profiles only after round-trip and conformance tests. [OneRoster](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/oneroster) · [QTI](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/qti) · [LTI](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/lti) · [CLR](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/clr)

### Application commands and failure semantics

Expose typed, versioned ConnectRPC application commands. The names here describe proposed behaviour. An importer, kiosk or webhook adapter calls the same authoritative service that the desk uses. Webhook/data-exchange endpoints follow the provider protocol, validate first, then call Go internally. Keep public storefront and student access purpose-scoped.

| Command | Input bound to authority | Durable result and replay rule |
|---|---|---|
| Add scan / amend basket | Session, lane, expected basket revision, stable scan-event key, identifier, measured quantity and unit if applicable | Accepted/unknown/restricted plus new revision. A transport replay returns the original result; two separate reads of the same SKU are legitimate. |
| Start tender / check existing tender | Confirmed amount/currency and remaining balance for start; attempt ID and authorised merchant binding for check | Start writes one attempt and an initiation result or unresolved case. Check has read/query semantics with respect to the provider charge, but can store evidence and reconcile locally. Rate-limit by merchant and attempt. |
| Resolve receipt / move to unapplied / refund | Verified provider evidence, application amount and remaining refundable balance, reason and required approval | Atomic receipt claim and allocation; unapplied funds tracked separately. Unknown refund outcomes have a new tracked attempt. A refund cannot exceed the remaining captured refundable amount. |
| Publish listings / import channel order | Approved publication revision, authorised seller/market, external order/event ID, source payload hash | Outbound job with per-variant results. Inbound conversion checks existing external order before creating another. Evidence of an earlier webhook can precede a successful pull. |
| Complete Flow / assign conversation | One-time session, allowed command, expected source revision, observed channel binding and verified response | Single authorised mutation; stale options require review. Human takeover suspends automatic outbound actions for the conversation. |
| Save answer / submit attempt / publish results | Enrolled identity, permitted time window/accommodation, attempt and response revision; grade release requires independent applicable Role | Per-answer acknowledgment, exactly one final submission, versioned assessment results and a reproducible issued transcript. A duplicate submit returns the original receipt without extending exam time. |

**Operations and readiness.** State progresses through Installed → Configuration incomplete → Sandbox verified → Awaiting external approval if required → Live verified → Degraded or Suspended. Show exact capability, owner and remediation at each state. Disabling new outbound charges or messages preserves the ability to reconcile already-started effects. Detect expiring credentials, stale inventory, unprocessed callbacks, excess unknown tenders, failed transcript signing and a non-empty marking queue. Show a recovery screen for each. Reconcile bank and provider statements to immutable attempts, and report orphan callbacks/chargebacks against the correct merchant. Record rollback compatibility and preserve provider/grade/Flow versions required by in-flight work.

### Pilot evidence required before a “ready to use” claim

1. **Retail lane and people.** A cashier completes a rush basket with repeated identical items, a damaged barcode, weighed produce, one mispriced shelf item and an age-check intervention. A second operator takes over the parked basket and finishes it with intact approvals. Measure recovery, queue impact and perceived workload.
2. **Payments.** Simulate timeout before provider ID, late successful callback, two overlapping status requests, manual C2B receipt claim at different lanes, customer transacting twice and terminal power loss. Finish the KES 1,350 fixture above. No unsupported “mark paid” or second charge path should be available.
3. **Kiosk.** One attendant receives multiple alerts, claims the correct lane and hands one shopper to staffed checkout. Restart a kiosk after payment; receipt and inventory remain single. Test keyboard-only, reduced reach, privacy reset and an unavailable handset.
4. **Commerce.** Publish one product with two variants, reject one channel listing, sell the last item at a till while a web shopper checks out, refund one line, process a duplicated marketplace order and reconcile commission. Demonstrate the fallback for a seller without native shop eligibility.
5. **Messaging.** Run one permitted Instagram comment-to-private-reply conversion and one WhatsApp substitution Flow against authorised test accounts. Revoke permission before a scheduled message, rotate the endpoint key and duplicate the completion. Each outcome is attributable and recoverable.
6. **Learning.** Verify the 74→77 grade correction and transcript chain. Test a staff/learner identity with two roles, interrupted submission and an expired guardian relationship. Export/import a supported assessment standard if advertised.
7. **Release record.** Keep date, version, Workspace profile, hardware/firmware, merchant market and scopes, test data, expected/actual, recovery time and named reviewer. No merchant credentials, payment execution, classroom data or live field observation were used for this research. Provider behaviour, account approval, performance and ease of use need qualification on the actual deployment.
