# Athena ERP • Research and design audit

20 November 2026 · Research evidence and proposed acceptance criteria · [Product playbook](Athena-ERP-Playbook.md) · [Starter catalogue and movements](Records-and-movements.md)

## What the evidence answers

A purchasing clerk can enter a bill with the correct total and still clear the wrong purchase order line. A finance team can produce a balanced trial balance and still have the wrong inventory valuation. An administrator can choose a sensible Role and accidentally grant access at another branch. These are three different failures. A metadata-driven form solves none of them on its own.

The two supplied Markdown documents establish the design baseline. The brief in the request supplies the research questions. The comparison below uses vendor material for product mechanics, then uses first-person questions and issue reports to choose failure cases. The sample favours accounting, inventory, access and deployment. It supports test design and product hypotheses. It supplies neither market share nor defect frequency. The audit is a desk review of specifications and a changing local checkout. All release gates below still need execution against a running release candidate.

## What an ERP actually stores

| System and unit of observation | Mechanism evidenced by its publisher | Decision for Athena |
|---|---|---|
| Frappe Framework and ERPNext | A DocType describes data and form structure. Permissions apply through users and roles. ERPNext describes reversal entries on cancellation and the cost consequences of backdated stock. | Generate desk and typed validation from Record Types. Give posting, cancellation and cost correction separate kernel contracts. Record Type authors should never have to implement double-entry safety. [Frappe DocType](https://docs.frappe.io/framework/user/en/basics/doctypes) · [Users and permissions](https://docs.frappe.io/framework/user/en/basics/users-and-permissions) · [ERPNext immutable ledger](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/immutable-ledger-in-erpnext) |
| SAP S/4HANA Finance | The Universal Journal brings financial and management accounting dimensions together at line-item grain in ACDOCA. In procurement the goods receipt/invoice receipt clearing account can stay open across several transactions. | Put company, account, book, currency and analytical dimensions on a common financial posting line. Retain separate physical stock movements. Give a controller a receipt-level explanation for uncleared amounts. [SAP Universal Journal](https://learning.sap.com/courses/outlining-cost-management-and-profitability-analysis/discussing-the-concept-of-the-universal-journal_f62cf151-5562-44f5-8ff6-df16fb232e94) · [SAP GR/IR reconciliation](https://learning.sap.com/courses/exploring-foundations-of-logistics-invoice-verification-in-sap-s-4hana-cloud-private-edition/explaining-gr-ir-account-maintenance-in-logistics-invoice-verification) |
| Dynamics 365 Business Central inventory | Item ledger entries record quantity, value entries record cost, and item application entries connect incoming and outgoing quantities or costs. Fixed applications support specific returns and corrections. | Persist which receipt supplied an issue and which issue a return reverses. Late freight can then split between goods on hand and goods already sold. The current Item price cannot answer this question. [Microsoft item application design](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/design-details-item-application) |
| Oracle NetSuite purchasing | The Approvals Workflow SuiteApp compares vendor bill, purchase order and receipt. Amount and quantity tolerances can independently route a discrepancy for review. | Show quantity, unit price, amount, currency, tax basis and tolerance separately. Record the reviewer, reason, policy version and matched lines. Block payment release while required exceptions remain open. [Oracle three-way match](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4096219721.html) |
| Odoo access and stock | Model access rights and record rules have different composition semantics. Odoo also documents valuation tracked by lot/serial. | Publish one explicit Grant evaluation contract. Preserve the lot through receipt, issue, return and drill-down. Accounting staff and warehouse staff can inspect the same movement at their own permitted level of detail. [Odoo security model](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/developer/reference/backend/security.html) · [Odoo lot valuation](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/inventory_valuation/valuation_by_lots.html) |
| ERPNext onboarding and assets | Opening invoices preserve receivable and payable detail. Opening stock has its own setup path. Depreciation uses an asset schedule. | Import unsettled items with dates, remaining amounts and currency. Bring in stock quantities and costs by location. Carry each asset’s remaining life and accumulated depreciation into its book. A lump-sum opening journal cannot reconstruct any of these. [Opening invoices](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/opening-invoice-creation-tool) · [Opening stock](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/opening-stock) · [Depreciation](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/asset-depreciation) |

The shared model is recognisable across the products. A master identifies the thing. An order records intent. A receipt records performance. An invoice recognises a claim. A settlement extinguishes it. These can occur on different dates, in different quantities, and in different currencies. A link between document headers alone loses the evidence needed to reconcile them.

## What operators asked for, and how to test Athena’s answer

| First-hand evidence | What it establishes | Proposed product response and falsifiable check |
|---|---|---|
| SAP Community user described two PO lines with the same material. Invoice quantities landed on the wrong line although totals matched. [SAP Community question and follow-up](https://community.sap.com/t5/enterprise-resource-planning-q-a/clearing-addressing-gr-ir-item-that-has-been-paid/qaa-p/7891056/highlight/true) | A real reconciliation question from 2011. It illustrates a durable accounting problem. | **A1** Match by line identity. Offer a reallocation/correction with approval and history. Equal header totals must leave two line-level exceptions visible until resolved. |
| An ERPNext v13 user reported a queued valuation rebuild after a backdated cancellation. The maintainer closed the issue after being unable to verify it. [ERPNext issue 25192](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/issues/25192) | A reported failure with explicit uncertainty. | **A2** Stop a rebuild halfway and restart it. Current reports show their watermark, the close cannot seal a pending valuation, and the prior signed report remains reproducible. |
| An Odoo user supplied concurrency cases involving accounting sequences, invoices and payments in versions 14/15. The issue is closed. [Odoo issue 91873](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/issues/91873) | A historical account of contention in workflows that share numbering. | **A3** Simultaneously submit two invoices within one series and replay one request. Expect two distinct numbers, two posting groups and one stable result per request. Test another company’s series independently. |
| A Frappe user reported an Analysis report timing out after applying a Company permission. Another user asked how to share certain reports without reconfiguring numerous DocTypes. [Scoped report performance](https://discuss.frappe.io/t/extremely-slow-query-when-user-has-permission-company-set/147828) · [Report permission question](https://discuss.frappe.io/t/exclude-user-permission-in-report/86861) | Users experience both latency and uncertainty about effective access. Forum explanations differ across report types and versions. | **A4** Compile Scope into the query before grouping. Scope two users differently and compare list, lookup, total, export and drill-down. Save the actual scoped query plan, cardinalities and timings. Make aggregate-only publication a separate reviewed capability if a customer needs it. |
| A travelling inventory user wanted to record changes while disconnected. A Frappe Books contributor separately requested offline POS synchronisation with ERPNext. [r/Odoo discussion, 2024](https://www.reddit.com/r/Odoo/comments/1gbcgjj) · [Frappe Books request 1174](https://github.com/frappe/books/issues/1174) | First-person requests for two distinct jobs. The Reddit post was available through the search index. An offline till also has payment, receipt and fiscal obligations. | **A5** Deliver offline count capture first. On reconnect, a revoked operator gets no posting, a duplicate upload gets one draft, and a changed stock baseline goes to review. Qualify a separate POS Pack for tender and shift reconciliation before promising offline sales. |
| An Odoo 15 issue describes an imported statement balance reflecting a later date. [Odoo issue 83459](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/issues/83459) | A historical example of why current balances and statement cutoff balances differ. | **A6** Import February before January, then reimport January. Both source statements keep their own opening and closing totals. Duplicate lines create no new payments. The residual resolves to identified unmatched rows. |

Prioritise A1, A3 and A4 for the first integrated trading Workspace. A2 becomes essential before accepting historical valuation changes. A5 earns its place through a field trial on actual devices. A6 gates the first bank reconciliation. To extend this research, tag cases by product, version, workflow, affected role, resolution and cost of recovery. Retain cases closed as expected behaviour. Interview a clerk and a controller about the same incident, collect anonymised source documents, then ask them to complete the identical scenario in Athena. Compare time, corrections, assistance and final balances. A claim of superiority needs that comparison.

## Audit of the two input specifications

| Priority | Design conflict or omission | Resolution in this edition | Acceptance evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Kernel §6 promises immutable movements and also describes rewriting historical valuations. | Freeze facts and Report Runs. Recompute derived Balances and append financial cost deltas with original economic date and permitted posting date. | Original movement hashes and old Report Run digest survive adjustment. New valuation and GL agree at the same cutoff. |
| P0 | UI §13 permits opening any Record as a developer. Kernel §21 separates platform and business access. | Build mode governs definition editing. Production data needs a scoped business assignment. Time-limited support access needs approval and an audit identity. | Developer can inspect a schema, cannot read payroll, and loses an approved support grant at expiry. |
| P0 | UI proposes immediate assignment and full-administrator safeguards. The draft RPC can set a user’s secret. | Save a reviewed change atomically. Protect the last active Instance Owner and privileged recovery. Use restricted first-login/reset challenges. | Race two owner removals, replay an invite, try ordinary RPCs before enrolment and verify revocation after reset. |
| P0 | UI makes CSV available everywhere and treats visibility as predictive of server acceptance. | Every read, export and attachment delivery gets fresh authorization. A stale screen can receive a denial or revision conflict. | Remove Scope while a report is queued. Verify delivery refusal and that an old link cannot request a new download. |
| P0 | Drafts list charts and stock accounts but leave matching grain and posting ownership ambiguous. | Each physical, financial and settlement event names its owning document and source line. Closing requires control account reconciliation. | Work the numerical scenario in the catalogue, then reverse dependent transactions and explain every balance. |
| P1 | Kernel mentions period close and year close together. Both carrying balances and posting opening rows can double-count. | Carry prior Balance as a derived opening. Reserve posted opening entries for cutover or a defined transfer between books. Monthly locking and fiscal-year closing have separate effects. | Opening plus net movement equals closing across two years, including a zero-activity account. |
| P1 | Two books depreciating one asset can double the GL expense. Custody changes need no monetary journal. | Map each book to an accounting book or mark it reporting-only. Only financial events produce GL. | Change custodian with no monetary effect. Run statutory and management books twice with one posting per posting-enabled book. |
| P1 | Attachment §24 references later detailed sections beyond the supplied file. | Supply line-level and Rule contracts, a seed catalogue, release gates and worked examples here. | First-party Pack authors implement the trading slice without inventing a new Kind, auth path or ledger write path. |
| P1 | Cloud and PWA choices imply different failure and recovery modes. | Keep the authoritative transaction local to the Workspace database. Optional services consume committed work. Limit first offline release to recoverable drafts. | Disable every optional provider during a local posting. Recover delayed sends and cached drafts without duplicating movements. |
| P1 | A catalogue of ERP screens can conceal an unworkable installation or upgrade. | Offer a Business starter setup with records ready to enter, an empty-core path, repeatable seeds, previewed changes and a restore drill. | Fresh install, rerun, interruption and upgrade yield identical definitions and retain customer values. |

## Release decision

Proceed with a trading and expenses pilot once bootstrap, authorization, posting and opening migration pass together. The sponsor should name a pilot company, jurisdiction, valuation method, accounting reviewer and recovery objectives before claiming production readiness. For payroll, statutory filing, bank payment initiation and consolidation, recruit the corresponding operator and validate a complete period with their real exceptions. Passing a generic journal fixture does not establish readiness in any of those domains.

## Where Athena could earn a measurable advantage

| Product hypothesis | Comparison protocol | Evidence that would disprove it |
|---|---|---|
| A warehouse supervisor can give a temporary receiver access to exactly one depot without contacting a developer. | Same two depots, inherited Roles and one expiry date in each product. Ask the supervisor to grant access, predict three allowed/denied actions, complete them as that user and revoke access. Capture wrong-scope grants, help requests and elapsed time. | A forbidden action succeeds, the supervisor cannot predict effective access, or the alternative is easier on the same task. |
| Finance can resolve a partial-receipt mismatch without a spreadsheet. | Give controllers the same two-line PO, misapplied invoice, part payment and credit. Ask them to find the residual and correct it. Check the resulting GRNI, AP, line quantities and audit history. | The system accepts a header-level match, requires a direct database change or obscures which lines the correction affects. |
| A field count survives interrupted connectivity without misleading the operator. | Same device class and count sheet. Disable networking at save and after server acknowledgement. Reopen, resolve a post-count movement, then sign out. Count lost drafts, duplicates and ambiguous statuses. | The UI says posted before acceptance, another user can see the draft, or stock changes twice. |
| A small team can leave a managed service and still reproduce its books. | Import a signed opening, complete a month, export and restore to self-hosted Athena without the vendor account. Rebuild Reports and follow document/attachment links. Record missing capabilities, downtime and unexplained residuals. | Any required data or key is vendor-only, an open item loses its due date, or the recovered period fails its financial controls. |

## Evidence to retain from the first pilot

Keep the exact Core/Pack versions, fixture or anonymised dataset manifest, device/browser, company/book/currency, granted Roles/Scopes and run date. For each acceptance case retain inputs, expected financial and operational outputs, observed output, linked request/posting IDs, reviewer and unresolved differences. For a failed case record the recovery steps actually needed. Preserve the pre-fix run so a later provider change, schema migration or optimisation can be compared against the same evidence.
