# Athena ERP • Industry Packs and operational controls

Research and design addendum • 20 November 2026 • Proposed contracts and acceptance cases

**A company should be able to install a few Packs, configure them from the desk, and run a complete business day.** A dairy cooperative needs to explain a farmer’s payout from individual deliveries. A contractor needs to pay a crew on Friday even when a camera failed on Tuesday. A restaurant needs to explain why last night’s cash, card settlement, ingredient consumption and sales report show four different amounts. Those are better tests of portability than the number of forms Athena can generate.

This volume extends the [product playbook](Athena-ERP-Playbook.md), [Record Type and movement catalogue](Records-and-movements.md) and [first research audit](Research-and-audit.md). The earlier volumes define the posting engine, Workspace roles, first-login flow, integrations and clean-install starter. This volume specifies what those foundations need to support across industries. It follows Athena’s existing naming. New business nouns below are proposed Record Types inside Packs. They are design proposals, separate from the capabilities observed in the Go checkout.

## 1. Ownership and composition

Keep one source of truth for each fact. A headcount belongs to an attendance event. Accepted square metres belong to a measurement certificate. Earned pay belongs to an approved earning line. Putting all three into a single “job complete” checkbox makes it impossible to correct one without silently changing the others.

| Owner | Responsibilities to design now | Examples of what a Pack supplies |
|---|---|---|
| Go Core | Workspace boundary, stable IDs, exact money and quantity, typed references, actor and device identity, revision checks, permissions, transaction, idempotency, event correlation, posting/reversal, bounded Rules, scheduling and durable evidence references. Add explicit lifecycle and configuration contracts below. | A Pack asks Go to post an approved payment or value transfer. Go checks book, scope, period, balance and duplicate identity on every call. |
| Shared first-party Packs | Stock and lot genealogy, accounts and settlement, Projects and job costing, Assets, People/Payroll, inspections, payment devices, private evidence and approvals. Define the owner of each movement before two Packs integrate. | Manufacturing and Butchery can both consume a lot and produce several outputs through the same stock movement contract. They need different data-entry forms and yield checks. |
| Industry Packs | Record Types, Modules, views, reports, business Roles, reference rows, workflows, effective-dated policies and Starlark Rules with declared capabilities. | A Dairy settlement may weight accepted litres by quality. A Construction certificate measures work against a bill of quantities. Neither industry formula belongs in Go. |
| Country Packs and adapters | Tax/fiscal receipts, payroll, reporting books, legal retention rules, payment-provider capabilities, local devices and data residency. Qualification is scoped to country, provider and tested version. | Kenya’s KRA describes separate system-to-system eTIMS options. Model fiscal submission, acceptance, rejection and correction separately from payment settlement. Validate the chosen option with the operator/integrator. [KRA integration options](https://www.kra.go.ke/business/etims-electronic-tax-invoice-management-system/learn-about-etims/etims-system-to-system-integration) |
| Company, site and device Settings | Select allowed variants within installed capabilities. Bind company, location, warehouse, default accounts, approval authorities, currency, business-day cutoff and secret references. | Two outlets share recipes but can have different prices and kitchen printers. A branch manager may change a printer routing rule without acquiring payroll or treasury privileges. |

**Installation contract.** A profile selects versioned Packs and names required capabilities, optional dependencies, fixture tests and Reports. Resolve compatible versions and any competing writers; preview tables, new grants and migrations; install under the existing Workspace lock. A capability has one financial posting owner per event, company and book. Save the resolved dependency graph with the installation. Running setup again preserves edited mappings. Removing a Pack first identifies open work, legal holds, required history and dependent Reports. Archive a retired process while preserving read access to posted references. Production upgrades must pass examples from at least two Packs that share the affected capability.

| Installation profile | First-party capabilities selected | What the owner must configure before use |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant with one or more outlets | Selling, Stock, Accounts, POS, Restaurant. Add Purchasing, prep production or delivery-channel connector as needed. | Outlet warehouse, business day, terminal/drawer, merchant, tender clearing, menu/recipe versions, price lists, tax and stock-consumption trigger. |
| Dairy cooperative | Parties, Buying, Stock, Quality, Accounts, Dairy. Optional herd management, dispatch and processing. | Farmer IDs, collection routes, approved units and instruments, rate policy, reject/hold location and payout calendar. |
| Poultry farm with hatchery | Stock, Buying, Selling, Accounts, Poultry and Hatchery. Manufacturing optional for feed or processing. | House and flock identities, count unit, egg classes, lot flow, placement and dispatch sites, ownership at each transfer. |
| Butchery and meat processing, with optional retail counter | Buying, Stock, Quality, Manufacturing transformation, Butchery and Accounts. Add POS for retail or Selling for wholesale dispatch. | Traceable suppliers, weighing units and scales, cutting/yield definitions, stock locations, shrink accounts and checkout prices. |
| Construction contractor | Projects, Stock, Buying, Evidence, People, Payroll, Accounts and Construction. Add Assets for owned equipment and owner-built assets. | Client contract versus own asset, site/crew calendars, pay basis, measurement units, signers, job-cost codes and holdbacks. |
| Manufacturer | Stock, Buying, Accounts, Manufacturing, Quality. Add Assets/Maintenance and subcontracting if needed. | Released BOMs and routings, work centres, approved substitutes, cost method, material ownership, inspection and variance disposition. |

**Three shared reference structures.** An *allocation* connects two source lines with a quantity, unit or amount, purpose, book and reversal link. A *transformation* connects several identified inputs with several outputs under a process ID, location, owner and effective/recorded times. An *observation* records who or what measured something, the value, unit, device and review status. These are proposed shared models with typed business fields. They support pay distribution, project-to-asset transfer, milk pooling, cutting, recipes and production without a universal free-form payload. GS1 EPCIS provides a useful external vocabulary for event exchange and traceability; map to it at the integration boundary. [GS1 EPCIS implementation guideline](https://ref.gs1.org/guidelines/epcis-cbv/2.0.0/)

## 2. Record lifetimes and disposal

**Kind, business lifetime and retention are independent.** A till shift lasts hours, its posted cash movements may need years of evidence, and its terminal session credential may expire in minutes. A closed construction project can retain verified quantities after raw worker video reaches its disposal date. Give each Type a declared business state, time basis, retention trigger, retention class, disposal action, allowed archive readers and hold behaviour. Go should expose these to generated screens, migrations, exports and background jobs.

| Lifetime profile | Examples and existing Kind | End of active use | What survives, and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-lived identity | Asset, employee, farmer Party, animal, site, machine, Item — Master | Departure, disposal, closure or archive | Stable identity and links for historical reports. Restrict or de-identify personal attributes under the applicable policy; freeze relevant business values on documents. |
| Effective-dated reference | Pay rate, recipe revision, burden rate, price, tax treatment — Master or Setting | Superseded prospectively by another version | Old version, applicability and who approved it. Every payroll, sale or posting cites the version it used. |
| Time-bounded engagement | Employment assignment, customer contract, lease, production campaign — Master or Document as declared | Contract or assignment ends | Obligations, balances, claims and amendments can outlive the engagement. Expire access separately. |
| Short-lived work | Order ticket, evidence draft, open shift, count sheet, work order — Document | Completed, abandoned, expired or cancelled | A completed ticket retains meaningful history. Abandoned drafts follow a shorter disclosed cleanup policy and cannot delete a posted dependent Record. |
| Event or observation | Check-in, milk weighing, temperature reading, till event — submitted Document or system Log by writer | Instant of capture plus review window | Original observation plus corrections, device and clock confidence. Aggregation/downsampling has an explicit loss-of-detail policy. A sensor reading alone cannot post wages or pass inspection. |
| Posting, application and settlement | Wage accrual, stock movement, payment application, asset transfer — Ledger and source Document | Reconciled or reversed | Append financial corrections, retain linkage and legal book evidence. A business reversal and privacy disposal are different operations. |
| Periodic result | Payslip, daily sales Report Run, supplier statement, Closing Pack — Snapshot | Issued, superseded or archived | Immutable output, input cutoffs, entitlement and digest. A revised run links to the original. Restrict read access independently of the operator who generated it. |
| Derived working state | Stock Bin — Balance; search projection or availability cache — infrastructure | Invalidated or rebuilt | Source Ledger and rebuild watermark. Refresh or delete the projection without changing the source. Bound replay windows for consumer deduplication. |
| Evidence media and renditions | Work video, image, signed delivery, thumbnail — attachment referenced by a Document | Validation, dispute and retention milestones | Schedule original and derivatives by class. A legal hold spans all covered versions. After authorised disposal retain a minimal receipt where permitted; do not claim that a digest restores the evidence. |
| Transient security/transport data | Activation challenge, session, upload intent, device pairing, job lease — restricted system state | Used, expired, revoked or fenced | Invalidate immediately. Retain a minimal Access Log event under policy. Keep long-lived business-operation identity when deleting an expired transport token. |

**A deletion workflow that can survive restore.** Select policy by jurisdiction, company, data class, purpose and contract. Record the start event (for example termination, fiscal close or dispute resolution). Preview candidates, block held items and dependent evidence, obtain any required review, erase or anonymise in all applicable primary stores, indexes, renditions, device caches and exports under operator control, then record outcome and exceptions. Backups expire on their own schedule. Reapply the disposal register before reopening a restored backup; propagate legitimate holds to rotated archives. A downloaded file outside the operator’s control has to be managed under the recipient agreement. Avoid putting names, photos, bank details or secrets in mandatory append-only audit payloads. Keeping personal data requires a purpose and a defensible retention period; local obligations determine actual durations. [ICO storage limitation guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/)

**Acceptance case.** Close a project with a worker dispute over one shift. Place the specific evidence under hold; let an unrelated draft upload expire. Restore yesterday’s backup into isolation. Expect the held evidence to be readable only by the case reviewers, the expired draft to stay unavailable after disposal reconciliation, and the approved payroll total to remain reproducible without exposing the worker’s photo to finance.

## 3. Encryption, key custody and secrets

Start with three explicit threats: a copied database or backup, data crossing an untrusted network, and a person or process with excessive access to decrypted data. Storage encryption addresses the first, authenticated transport addresses the second, and scoped identities, isolation and audit address the third. Document who can decrypt what in a self-hosted installation and a hosted Workspace. Avoid promising end-to-end encryption for content the server must decrypt to validate, post, search or render.

| Data path / store | Proposed baseline | Test and operator-visible evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Browser/PWA, CLI or device → edge → Go | HTTPS for remote access, TLS 1.3 preferred with reviewed TLS 1.2 compatibility as needed. Verify service identity and hostname on each network hop, including edge-to-origin. Restrict trusted proxy headers. Authenticate device bridges separately; mutually authenticate managed service links where the deployment requires it. | Inventory endpoints and certificate owners. Exercise renewal; untrusted peer, wrong hostname or expired certificate prevents the connection. Show expiry and rotation health without exposing key material. [OWASP TLS guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Transport_Layer_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html) |
| Go/worker → MariaDB, object store, secrets service or external POS | Trusted CA and peer verification for remote database and API connections. Prefer a protected Unix socket for same-host DB where appropriate. Give worker, application, migration and backup processes different identities. Protect webhook authenticity and replay handling in addition to TLS. | Capture the effective connection mode and identity at readiness checks. Test that a remote database connection cannot silently fall back to plaintext. A connector outage queues work safely. [MariaDB secure connections](https://mariadb.com/docs/server/security/encryption/data-in-transit-encryption/secure-connections-overview) |
| Database volumes, replicas, logs, temp space and snapshots | Self-hosted deployment specifies volume/database encryption and exactly which logs, temp files and exports it covers. Review binary logs separately. On RDS select and inventory the KMS key, recovery dependencies and all copies. | A clean-machine restore uses explicitly authorised keys. Verify replicas, snapshots and exports separately. Record any unprotected store as a release blocker for its data class. [MariaDB binary-log encryption](https://mariadb.com/docs/server/security/encryption/data-at-rest-encryption/managing-binary-log-encryption) · [RDS encryption](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonRDS/latest/UserGuide/Overview.Encryption.html) |
| Private files, photos, video, reports and backups | Encrypt local blob volumes or provider objects, including derivatives and backup copies. Use Workspace-bound object IDs, private ACLs, authorisation on download and short-lived links. Use versioned digests for integrity. | An exported encrypted backup contains no plaintext sidecar with secrets or sensitive thumbnails. A revoked user cannot obtain a fresh URL. Previously issued links have a bounded residual lifetime; proxy highly sensitive downloads. |
| Selected fields requiring separation from database readers | Use vetted authenticated encryption through a narrow key-service interface. A data encryption key encrypts the value; a separately controlled key wraps it. Store algorithm/version, key reference, nonce and ciphertext, binding Workspace, Record and field context as authenticated data. Separate key domains by purpose and threat model. | Copy a protected value into a different Record and decryption must fail. Ordinary database readers see ciphertext. Document impact on joins, indexes, search, rotation and restore before enabling field protection. Keep ordinary ledger arithmetic in a secured transactional database. [OWASP cryptographic storage](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Cryptographic_Storage_Cheat_Sheet.html) |
| PWA, shared tills, printers and kiosks | Classify what may leave the server. Restrict device caches by user/Workspace, minimise offline PII, use managed-device disk encryption when local durable media is required. Disable persistent private caching on untrusted/shared devices. Printers and spoolers need a retention policy. | Account switch, device loss, kiosk restart and browser storage eviction have explicit outcomes. A web app cannot promise remote erasure while the device is disconnected. |
| Logs, metrics, support bundles and exports | Allowlist diagnostic fields. Redact DSNs, tokens, signatures, bearer URLs and raw evidence metadata before emission. Authorise CSV, scheduled Reports, thumbnails and backup download independently. | Search a synthetic support bundle for planted test secrets, exercise a restricted export, and verify published figures still explain themselves without sensitive payloads in telemetry. |

**Key lifecycle.** Maintain purpose, owner, affected Workspaces, key version, creation/rotation state, access audit and recovery location. Rotate a wrapping key by rewrapping data keys where supported; a compromised data key may require re-encrypting its data. Keep old decryption versions for authorised retained backups, then retire them after coverage verification. Use independent approval for destructive key deletion and escrow/recovery appropriate to the deployment. A restored SQL database alone cannot recover an encrypted video or bank instruction. Prove recovery on an isolated target before retiring the old key. [OWASP key management](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Key_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html)

### Secret storage and rotation

A Setting may contain a **secret reference** specifying provider, permissible purpose, environment and a version or approved alias. The secret value never enters normal Record CRUD, Pack source, a Starlark variable, URL, frontend response, configuration diff or Report. Passwords for Athena’s own users are one-way password hashes. API credentials, device secrets and third-party passwords that must be presented to another service require separate custody.

| Deployment | Bootstrap and storage | Rotation and failure policy |
|---|---|---|
| Local owner-operated | Installation uses a protected OS credential store or locked-down service-managed secret file/mount outside the Workspace repository, accessible only to the intended OS identity. Encrypt disks and separately protect recovery material. An ignored file is still plaintext to anyone with filesystem access. | Replace by version, reconnect using the new credential, then revoke the previous one. Verify backup recovery under a different host identity. Surface which dependency is unavailable without revealing its secret. |
| Self-hosted shared operations | A secrets service such as OpenBao can issue leased credentials to service identities. Restrict paths by environment, Workspace, role and connector; give the application only needed decrypt/read operations. | Rotate/release leases and recycle connection pools before expiry. Test secret-service restart, loss of quorum and restore of protected recovery material. [OpenBao database secrets](https://openbao.org/docs/secrets/databases/) |
| Managed AWS | Use workload identity to reach Secrets Manager/KMS. Separate control-plane, runtime and backup principals. A finance clerk can operate the Payment Pack without reading the provider credential. | Stage and validate a new version, use overlapping credentials only where the provider supports it, confirm new connections, then revoke old versions. Single-user and alternating-user rotation have different operational requirements. [AWS rotation strategies](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/secretsmanager/latest/userguide/rotation-strategy.html) |

The admin UI exposes **Connect**, **Test**, **Rotate**, **Revoke** and last successful use. Test only the intended destination and capability. For an incoming webhook, rotation can accept the previous signing key for a bounded period while deduplicating business events independently. On suspected compromise, disable the binding, revoke affected credentials, reissue with restricted scope and reconcile any uncertain external payments before retry. Allow an independently working subsystem such as local stock entry to continue when an optional email provider is down. [OWASP secrets management](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Secrets_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html)

## 4. Settings a company can change safely

Model each Setting with a stable key, owner Pack, data type, schema version, default, allowed scopes, merge semantics, validation, activation mode, effective time, sensitivity, approval requirement and audit policy. An operator should be able to answer “why is this branch charging this price today?” directly from the desk. Frappe documents site-specific and common configuration; AWS AppConfig documents controlled rollout and monitored rollback. Athena also needs historical business determinism. [Frappe site configuration](https://docs.frappe.io/framework/user/en/basics/site_config) · [AWS AppConfig rollback](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/appconfig/latest/userguide/monitoring-deployments.html)

| Class | Permitted owner and examples | Resolution behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Deployment/boot configuration | Platform operator selects DB endpoint, secret backend, TLS and backup target | Resolve before opening the Workspace. Allowlisted environment overrides only for operational keys. Store a redacted fingerprint. The desk shows source and health, with privileged changes through the operator interface. |
| Pack defaults and Workspace policy | Pack declares supported defaults. Instance Owner selects active Packs, identity, offline policy and locale defaults. | Apply schema-validated Workspace Overlay over pinned Pack version. Security floors and legal/book constraints constrain lower scopes. An explicit disable differs from inherit. |
| Company, site, device | Company selects base currency, fiscal calendar and pay frequency. Outlet selects trading-day cutoff or kitchen routing. Device selects printer or till. | A key explicitly allows its scope chain. For eligible scalar preferences use most-specific valid value. Lists declare replace/append/intersect; permissions require restrictive semantics. Conflicting candidates at equal priority are errors. |
| User preferences | Language, display density, saved filters | User can override presentation within policy. Base currency, tax treatment, Grant scope and overnight business date belong to authorised business policy. |
| Dated business reference | Contract rate, menu price, tax treatment, BOM revision, pay band | Store as a versioned reference Record with effective ranges, company, time zone and approval. Resolve at the declared event instant and freeze the selected revision on the transaction. |
| Feature activation and secret bindings | Canary UI, optional connector, allowed payment mode, credential reference | Feature availability never confers authority. A kill switch may stop new attempts; the recovery worker must still reconcile an already authorised payment. Secret values use the separate service above. |

**Change workflow.** Draft → validate type, dependency and jurisdiction constraints → preview the effective diff for specific sites, devices and future business dates → simulate against representative Records → approve → publish by revision → observe → supersede or roll back prospectively. Use optimistic revisions for concurrent edits. In one sale, payroll run or cost posting, resolve one consistent configuration generation. Store only relevant policy IDs and effective values with the event. Distinguish values which hot-reload, need a restart, require schema migration, or are locked after first posting. A new Pack default must not overwrite local values. A removed setting with active dependants blocks upgrade until remapped.

**Worked settings conflict.** A restaurant changes the lunch offer at noon. Outlet A permits it; Outlet B opts out. An accepted order at 11:59 retains its evaluated price after a 12:03 card retry. An edited basket is repriced visibly according to the edit policy. The refund references original line amounts. Rollback at 12:10 changes future resolution only. An offline device with an expired price book must display its limited mode and apply the permitted exception process. The test asserts four separate facts: offer version, branch eligibility, price charged and amount returned.

## 5. POS connections, devices and daily settlement

**Supermarket operating requirement.** Keep the basket and every confirmed tender leg visible when a device stalls. The cashier’s recovery action is **Check payment**, which queries the original attempt. A separate **Park sale** action releases the lane for the next shopper and leaves a named owner for the unresolved payment. Reprints, status checks and supervisor handoffs retain the same sale identity. A number supplied for an M-PESA prompt is scoped to that payment. On an STK recheck the adapter uses the stored CheckoutRequestID; an independently made Till/PayBill payment requires its own merchant-confirmed matching path. [Safaricom SDK](https://github.com/safaricom/mpesa-php-sdk/blob/master/src/Mpesa.php)

**Add cashier and attendant acceptance cases to the POS Pack.** Scan two identical items, type a damaged barcode, weigh produce, resolve a shelf-price discrepancy, request supervisor assistance, split cash/mobile tender and resume a held sale at another lane. A self-service kiosk adds a shopper shell, private payment entry and an attendant queue. Compare time waiting for the device with time waiting for staff. The companion volume specifies fields, recovery dialogs, frontend routes, configuration and tests. [Supermarket checkout, commerce channels and learning](Athena-Checkout-Commerce-and-Learning.md#cashiers)

There are three different integrations a merchant may call “connect my POS”. Identify which one owns order entry, which owns payment authorisation and which owns stock before installing anything.

| Deployment | Flow | Contract to qualify |
|---|---|---|
| Athena till and external card terminal | Athena owns basket, price, order, receipt and stock policy. It asks a paired terminal/provider to collect a definite amount for a merchant, device and currency. Provider returns a checkout/payment reference and asynchronous status. | Pairing and unpairing, operator/device permissions, supported regional tenders, cancel/refund, response loss, duplicate notification, settlement feed. Square’s Terminal API is an example of a separate POS application driving a terminal. [Square Terminal API](https://developer.squareup.com/docs/terminal-api/overview) |
| Existing POS feeding Athena | POS owns receipts, line items, discounts, tax, tender and void/refund facts. Athena imports those facts and posts agreed stock/finance events. Start in read-only reconciliation mode. | Map immutable outlet/register/receipt/line IDs, timezone, menu modifier, tax, tender, returns and original references. Support cursor + overlap, deduplication and daily provider-total comparison. Summary-only feeds can support financial reconciliation but cannot establish customer-level lot or recipe detail. Disclose this limitation at setup. |
| Local peripheral bridge | Pair a workstation with specific scales, barcode readers, receipt/kitchen printers, drawers or customer displays. Restrict commands to the site, assigned devices, supported operations and authenticated operator. | Register capabilities, firmware/protocol version, calibration or verification dates, health and last acknowledgement. Print jobs carry IDs; reprints are marked. Printer failure after payment routes the receipt for reprint without charging again. A scale must return unit, tare, stable reading and device identity. [GS1 retail barcode guidance](https://ref.gs1.org/guidelines/2d-in-retail/) |

**Proposed Records and identities.** Outlet, Register, Device, Merchant Binding and Tender Type are Masters or controlled Settings. Till Shift, Cash Movement, POS Invoice, Tender Attempt, Refund and Settlement Batch are Documents; receipt lines, tax allocations and tender splits are Children. A provider webhook inbox and durable outbox belong to Core infrastructure. Store merchant and outlet in the external identity key. The same receipt number can appear at two tills. A void before payment, a refund after capture, a stock return, a chargeback and a fiscal credit note have different effects and separate references.

**Payment state.** Start a tender attempt using an operation key and exact amount/currency. Track created, awaiting customer, authorised, captured, declined, cancelled and unknown, according to provider capabilities. Some providers combine authorisation and capture. Keep settlement, tip adjustment, refund and dispute as linked later events. After a timeout, look up the original attempt or await a verified notification before authorising a new charge. An out-of-order “authorised” notice cannot regress “captured”. A sale with a successful cash leg and an uncertain card leg remains visibly partly tendered. Release change, issue the appropriate receipt, account for tax and affect stock at each Pack’s declared and tested event. A simple success toast cannot decide any of these.

**Cash and card data.** Use the provider’s supported terminal/SDK, keep PAN entry and PIN handling inside it, and store only necessary provider references and permitted display metadata. Never collect card verification codes into Athena for later use. Validate PCI scope with the merchant’s acquirer for the actual deployment and hardware. Hardware-backed processing reduces exposure but does not by itself certify the ERP. [PCI SSC on card-verification codes](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/faqs/are-merchants-allowed-to-request-card-verification-codes-values-from-cardholders/)

**Offline contract.** The existing PWA policy starts with drafts. Qualify offline selling per device, tender and jurisdiction separately. A cash till can record a locally numbered provisional sale under an explicit device policy and reconcile to the server with a permanent event ID. Offline card acceptance requires documented support for that exact SDK, reader and region, with amount/age/exposure limits, explicit merchant risk acceptance, retained provider references, and recovery for transactions declined after reconnection. The generic browser queue never stores card credentials. A QR or mobile-money request remains pending until the provider confirms the reference and amount. Where fiscal issuance mandates connectivity or certified software, configure the supported workflow or prevent final issuance. [Stripe offline reader/platform matrix](https://docs.stripe.com/terminal/features/operate-offline/collect-card-payments)

**Drawer and bank close fixture.** Opening float 100. Sales before tax 300, tax 30, cash tender 120 and card tender 210. Return 22 in cash against an earlier cash sale (20 revenue and 2 tax in this fixture); recorded cash drop 50. Expected drawer is 148. A physical count of 147 creates a separately approved shortage of 1. Net revenue is 280; net tax 28; net tenders 308. The card clearing account is 210 until the acquirer settles 204 and charges 6. A cash drop is a transfer of custody; treating it as a refund would understate sales. The daily Report must bridge receipt totals → tenders → drawer or clearing → deposit and fees by trading day. Assign tips, service charges, gift-card liability, split tender, partial refunds and aggregator commissions distinct accounts and totals when enabled. A refund after midnight keeps both the original sale link and the refund’s own trading day.

**Go-live break tests.** Close during a shift handover; attempt a second close; disconnect after customer approval; refund a split-tender sale; process a partial settlement three days later; replace a paired device with outstanding attempts; run two tills against the last available ingredient. Define who may open, float, void, refund, authorise an exceptional discount, count cash and approve variances. The same cashier cannot silently approve their own unexplained shortage. A provisional close identifies unresolved attempts by ID and mandates follow-up.

## 6. Construction: from daily work to pay, billing and assets

### A foreman’s day and a payroll clerk’s Friday

1. **Plan.** Project → site → work breakdown/task → bill of quantities line. Set contract, crew, supervisor, unit, budget and applicable pay agreement. Record drawing/specification revision, approved change order, weather/delay codes and whether the work is client work, maintenance or construction of the company’s own asset.
2. **Roster and check in.** Publish a dated shift with planned workers or subcontract crew. Record actual check-in/out, breaks, site, task, device/local time, server receipt time and any supervisor correction. Badge, manual register and authorised offline check-in are alternatives. A roster shows what was planned; attendance records who was present.
3. **Record delivered work.** A foreman records a measurement such as 32 m² against a particular wall section and drawing revision. Link one or more private photos/videos, a delivery docket, inspection or signed measurement. Track contributors and hours separately from quantity. Check for overlapping claims against the same area and prior certified quantities. Procore’s Daily Log distinguishes manpower, daily construction reports, quantities, photos and delays. [Procore Daily Log](https://support.procore.com/products/online/user-guide/project-level/daily-log/tutorials/daily-log-overview)
4. **Review.** A supervisor or quantity surveyor accepts all or part, rejects with reason or asks for correction. Independent review can use site inspection or witness attestation when media is missing. Store claimed, accepted, rejected, disputed and remaining quantities with identities, reason and date. An approved certificate can support progress billing without changing attendance.
5. **Prepare payroll.** The pay rule identifies time-, day-, piece- or mixed-rate work, ordinary/overtime, pay calendar, approved rate revision and permitted deductions. Check for overlapping hours, duplicate attendance, double allocation of the same earning, rounding and missing rate. Route exceptions to payroll with a deadline and a person responsible. Show the worker the basis and a correction channel. Missing photographs trigger review; an attachment failure must never automatically forfeit time wages. Local labour law and agreements govern entitlements, due dates and adjustments. [ILO protection of wages](https://www.ilo.org/ilo-helpdesk-questions-and-answers-business-wages-and-benefits)
6. **Pay on the scheduled day.** Keep work date, earned period, cutoff, scheduled payday, actual settlement date and bank reference. Approve the run, accrue wages/payables by job or cost code, then initiate payment under separate treasury authority. If some workers are unpaid or a transfer outcome is unknown, display each outcome and reconcile before retrying. A subsequent correction creates a linked adjustment run. ## 10. Optional poultry extension — hatchery

Hatchery is an optional extension for poultry operators who incubate eggs. A hatchery can be standalone, buy eggs from many suppliers, or receive eggs from the farm’s breeder flock. Its work centres and batches are reusable manufacturing concepts; its cohort identities and outcome denominators belong in Hatchery. Breeder fertility and hatch outcomes measure different things. [Mississippi State University Extension on breeder fertility](https://extension.msstate.edu/publications/the-only-good-broiler-breeder-egg-fertilized-egg)

**Grain and flow.** Source flock or supplier lot → received egg lot → graded/eligible lot → setting batch and tray allocation → machine/location transfer → observed results → hatch outcome → chick grading, sale, internal placement or other disposition. Store source quantity, actual accepted count, batch, tray, dates, device/operator, observation method, outcome confidence and approved cost allocation. Track third-party ownership when hatching on contract. A mixed machine load needs per-source tray records if source-level performance is promised; otherwise report only at the observed mixed-lot grain.

**Worked hatch.** Set 1,000 eggs. Under the fixture’s reviewed classification, 900 were fertile, 100 infertile, and 810 chicks hatched. Of those, 790 are saleable and 20 have another recorded disposition. Hatch of eggs set = 81%; hatch of fertile eggs = 90%; saleable chicks per egg set = 79%. The remaining 90 fertile eggs are accounted for as unhatched outcomes. When fertility is unknown for some eggs, report the unknown count and method rather than imputing a definitive result. Prevent both a chick sale and an internal placement for the same allocated quantity. The daily dashboard shows batches due, output by source, unexplained losses, dispatch commitments and dealer/customer balances.

### One company runs a butchery and a restaurant

A combined business is a harder proof than two independent demos. Use one Workspace, one legal company, one chart, two outlets, separate warehouse locations and two independent cash drawers. Install Butchery and Restaurant together. Bind both to the same Stock Item and lot identities. The following fixture uses an internal transfer at carrying value, a recipe with one costed ingredient, no tax, and cash sales. It isolates the shared posting contract.

1. The cutting room receives 20 kg of beef at 5 per kg. Debit Stock 100, credit GRNI 100 under the starter receipt policy. Cut it into 12 kg counter cuts and 8 kg kitchen trim. For this fixture allocate cost by weight. Counter value is 60 and trim value is 40.
2. Transfer the 8 kg trim to the restaurant. The source location decreases 8 and the destination increases 8 under one transfer identity. Company stock value stays 100. The butchery manager sees dispatch, the chef sees receipt and finance sees no internal sale. A different legal owner would require the intercompany workflow.
3. The butchery sells 4 kg for 40 cash. The restaurant uses 3 kg for 12 plates and takes 72 cash. The declared consumption trigger relieves the restaurant stock once at 15 cost. The retail receipt relieves 20 cost. Two POS sessions reconcile independently.
4. Close with 8 kg counter cuts valued at 40 and 5 kg kitchen trim valued at 25. Combined stock is 65; COGS 35; revenue 112; gross profit 77. Cash received is 40 at one till and 72 at the other. A report adding internal transfers to revenue or issuing the 3 kg at both preparation and sale fails the fixture.
5. Place the incoming lot under a retrospective recall. Both outlets identify remaining affected stock and original sales, with the limits of customer identification shown for walk-in sales. A restricted cashier can withdraw the items from sale without viewing supplier costs or customer data from the other outlet.

This same test should still pass after an independent upgrade of either Pack, a printer retry, or a scheduled sales Report run. It demonstrates the practical meaning of an installable Pack: aggregate reports and stock movements still agree when two businesses share them.

### A proof sequence for the roadmap

1. **Freeze the cross-Pack contracts.** Choose ownership and identity of a source-line application, lot transformation, observation, evidence reference, terminal attempt, scoped setting revision and retention policy. Add schema version and compatibility fixtures. An acceptance suite should exercise the same Go services through Vue, CLI, import and background job. The prior roadmap is kernel-first; these proposals supply its fixtures and a specification for later Packs.
2. **Prove two contrasting installations.** Start one Restaurant and one Construction Workspace. The first must close a real trading day with stock and settlement; the second must reconcile a pay run and partial commissioning. Together they expose physical, digital, privacy and accounting boundaries without promising every Pack at once.
3. **Prove custody and genealogy across three businesses.** Use Dairy pooling, Butchery cutting and mixed-lot processing, and Poultry flock transfers. Each operator should be able to trace one disputed unit or amount upstream and downstream. Capture missing evidence and unknown lots explicitly. Add discrete manufacturing and subcontracting as the next test of the same model.
4. **Test deployment failure and portability.** Run the same scenarios once self-hosted and once using the chosen managed profile. Rotate a secret during live work; let a certificate expire in a test environment; restore an encrypted backup with a held attachment; disable one optional provider. Record the business actions that continue, fail closed, or require operator review. Run outward payments and fiscal submissions in provider test environments before any live qualification.
5. **Qualify each market.** The pilot owner supplies operating country, contracts, mandatory reports, devices, connectivity, languages, wage agreements, accounting framework, payment providers and retention basis. Publish support by Pack version, deployment, country, device and tested workflow. Use status such as designed, implemented, field-tested and qualified, each linked to actual evidence.
